| **Strategy Name**           | **Description**                                                                                  | **Prompt Addition** |
|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| `story_wisdom`              | Narrative-style walkthrough with storytelling and personal insights                             | Structure the summary as a story-driven walkthrough. Emphasize the author’s journey, key experiences, and lessons. Use a personal, reflective tone. |
| `framework_stepwise`        | Step-by-step process or structured method explanations                                           | Summarize using a clear step-by-step structure. Highlight each phase, principle, or rule in the order the book presents it. Name and explain each step thoroughly. |
| `insight_listing`           | List of deep insights or ideas, like condensed wisdom                                            | Create a themed list of core insights. Introduce each one with a short narrative or metaphor. The summary should feel like distilled wisdom. |
| `comparison_reflection`     | Reflective summaries that compare ideas (e.g., old vs new mindsets)                             | Use a compare-and-contrast structure. Reflect on contrasting mindsets or approaches. Frame ideas as old vs new, traditional vs evolved, etc. |
| `tactical_breakdown`        | Actionable, tactical guide with implementation-ready tips                                       | Break the summary into tactical sections. Focus on what the reader should do or apply. Use practical language and highlight real-world utility. |
| `philosophical_reflection` | Thoughtful and interpretive — often abstract, idea-focused narratives                           | Focus on big ideas and thoughtful interpretation. Allow for slower pacing, abstract thinking, and deeper reflections. Avoid bullet points. |
| `case_study`                | Uses stories/examples to illustrate ideas — usually research-based or narrative-driven          | Highlight key examples and real-life stories. Each section should focus on a different case or story that teaches a broader idea. |
| `visual_framework`          | Emphasizes mental models, diagrams, or structural frameworks                                     | Frame the summary around visual or mental models. Even though images aren’t available, describe frameworks clearly with analogies. |
| `motivational_manifesto`    | Persuasive, uplifting summaries that motivate the reader — more about mindset than how-to        | Make the tone inspirational and energizing. Summarize ideas with emotional power and persuasive rhythm, like a rallying call. |
| `interview_or_collation`    | Built around interviews, multi-author entries, or curated wisdom from others                    | Summarize each major contributor’s insight or story. Clearly attribute ideas if names are mentioned. Keep the format modular and digestible.
