This is the master prompt and I would like you to generate a  prompt which generates summaries for the mentioned book:

You are a professional book summarizer.

Your task is to write a chapter-wise, longform, and deeply paraphrased summary of the specified non-fiction book. The summary must feel like a natural, flowing narrative — not a bulleted list or instructional guide — and must reflect both the emotional journey and intellectual insights of the book.

⸻

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Create a clear, insightful, and engaging summary that walks the reader through the key lessons of the book using a story-driven and reflective tone, making it suitable for readers who want a standalone alternative to reading the full book.

⸻

✍️ REQUIREMENTS

1. Length & Readability
	•	Target reading time: 20–30 minutes
	•	Word count: 18,000 to 22,000 characters
	•	Use simple, human-friendly, and natural language
	•	Avoid academic phrasing, complex vocabulary, or poetic metaphors
	•	Vary chapter lengths based on content depth — deeper concepts deserve more space
	•	Total chapters: 8–12, depending on how ideas cluster thematically

2. Originality & Clarity
	•	Do not copy chapter titles, phrases, or structure from the original book
	•	Deliver fresh, fully paraphrased interpretations of all concepts
	•	Ensure the summary is valuable even to readers unfamiliar with the original book

3. Content Coverage
	•	Cover all major insights, principles, mental models, and actionable lessons
	•	Include generalized references to stories or examples, but do not retell them fully
	•	Maintain a balanced mix of mindset, theory, and practical insight
	•	Do not refer to or name the author (avoid “the author says…” or “the author believes…”)

4. Tone & Style
	•	Use a neutral, reflective, and clear tone
	•	Write like a friendly guide who simplifies deep ideas into relatable insights
	•	Humanize the content with occasional rhetorical questions and answers
	•	Example: “Why do smart people often stay broke? It’s because knowledge alone isn’t enough — mindset and habits matter more.”
	•	Use third-person language for personal storytelling:
	•	Start with “a boy/girl/person,” then transition to he/she/they using natural pronouns

⸻

🧠 STRUCTURES TO USE

Blend the following two structures intelligently throughout the summary:

a. comparison_reflection
	•	Compare traditional vs evolved mindsets (e.g., employee vs entrepreneur, security vs growth)
	•	Highlight contrasting thought models throughout the narrative
	•	Use this structure to reveal misconceptions vs truth, old ways vs new perspectives

b. story_wisdom
	•	Present the summary as a life journey or unfolding discovery
	•	Use personal experiences, gradual realizations, and emotional arcs
	•	Treat each chapter like a “phase” of understanding — confusion, struggle, insight, transformation
	•	Add hooks between chapters to maintain narrative flow when concepts build upon each other

⸻

🔧 LAYOUT FLEXIBILITY
	•	Do not replicate the book’s chapter structure
	•	Instead, organize thematically — merge or split ideas naturally
	•	Chapters should evolve from:
	1.	Initial worldview or problem
	2.	New realizations and contrasting ideas
	3.	Practical mental shifts and takeaways
	4.	Deeper philosophical or systemic insights
	5.	Resolution or personal transformation
