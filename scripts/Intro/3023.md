## Introduction

Warnings from history echo through the present, revealing how fragile democratic institutions can be when confronted by the forces of authoritarianism. The lessons of the twentieth century, marked by the rise and fall of tyrannical regimes, offer urgent reminders about the subtle and overt ways in which freedom can be eroded. In On Tyranny: Twenty Lessons from the Twentieth Century, Timothy Snyder draws from the turbulent past to illuminate the patterns and warning signs that often precede the loss of liberty. The specter of tyranny is not confined to distant lands or bygone eras; it can emerge anywhere complacency and indifference take root.

Through a series of concise, powerful lessons, the narrative weaves together historical examples and practical guidance, urging vigilance and moral clarity in the face of creeping authoritarianism. Each lesson serves as both a reflection on the past and a call to action, emphasizing the importance of individual responsibility, critical thinking, and civic engagement. The journey through these lessons reveals not only the dangers that threaten open societies but also the enduring power of ordinary people to resist and defend democratic values. With each insight, the path forward becomes clearer, inviting a deeper understanding of what it means to safeguard freedom in uncertain times.
