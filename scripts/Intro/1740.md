## Introduction

The journey of faith often unfolds as a dynamic, ever-evolving relationship with the divine, where questions of purpose, trust, and transformation take center stage. In the world of Running with God: A discipleship guide to grow in faith and experience the power and love of God by Liz Hill-O'Shea, the pursuit of spiritual growth becomes an invitation to walk—and sometimes run—alongside a loving, powerful presence. The longing to experience God’s love more deeply and to witness tangible expressions of His power shapes the path, encouraging a life marked by both surrender and boldness. Discipleship emerges not as a static checklist, but as a vibrant, ongoing process of becoming, where faith is both tested and strengthened through real-life challenges and moments of grace.

As the journey unfolds, practical wisdom and heartfelt encouragement illuminate the way, offering guidance for those seeking to deepen their relationship with God. The rhythms of prayer, scripture, and community become essential companions, fostering resilience and hope in the face of doubt or difficulty. Through stories, reflections, and actionable steps, the call to grow in faith is woven together with the promise of encountering God’s transformative love and power. The invitation is clear: step forward with courage, embrace the adventure, and discover what it truly means to run with God.
