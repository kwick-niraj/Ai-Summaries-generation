## Introduction

The invisible wounds of war often linger long after the battlefield has faded from view, shaping lives in ways that are both profound and deeply personal. In the world of Until Tuesday: A Wounded Warrior and the Golden Retriever Who Saved Him by Luis Carlos Montalván, the journey of healing unfolds through the unlikely partnership between a decorated veteran and a devoted service dog. The story navigates the complexities of trauma, resilience, and the transformative power of unconditional companionship, revealing how the bond between human and animal can become a lifeline in the darkest of times.

As the narrative weaves through the challenges of post-traumatic stress disorder and the struggle to reclaim a sense of purpose, the presence of Tuesday, the golden retriever, emerges as a beacon of hope and stability. The evolving relationship between man and dog becomes a testament to trust, patience, and the small victories that mark the path toward recovery. Through moments of vulnerability and courage, the experience highlights the profound impact of empathy and connection, inviting reflection on the ways healing can be found in the most unexpected places.
