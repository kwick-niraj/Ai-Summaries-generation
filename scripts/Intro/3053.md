## Introduction

In the dialogues and philosophical treatises attributed to Plato, the search for truth, justice, and the nature of reality unfolds through vivid conversations and probing questions. The world of Plato: Complete Works invites engagement with the foundational ideas that have shaped Western thought for centuries—questions about the ideal society, the nature of virtue, the immortality of the soul, and the pursuit of knowledge. Through the interplay of Socratic questioning and the exploration of metaphysical forms, the boundaries between appearance and reality, opinion and knowledge, are continually tested and redefined.

As the dialogues progress, the voices of Socrates, Glaucon, Phaedrus, and others weave together a tapestry of philosophical inquiry that ranges from the ethics of individual conduct to the structure of the cosmos. The Republic, Symposium, Phaedo, and other works offer not only theoretical frameworks but also practical reflections on love, friendship, governance, and the cultivation of wisdom. Each conversation builds upon the last, inviting deeper reflection on the meaning of a good life and the responsibilities of citizenship. The journey through these works becomes an invitation to question, to reason, and to seek understanding in the company of some of history’s most enduring minds.
