## Introduction

Invisible forces often shape the course of politics, steering public policy and national discourse in ways that remain largely unseen. In the world Jane Mayer uncovers in Dark Money: The Hidden History of the Billionaires Behind the Rise of the Radical Right, the immense influence of a select group of wealthy individuals comes to light. These billionaires, operating behind the scenes, have quietly funneled vast sums into think tanks, advocacy groups, and political campaigns, all with the aim of shifting the ideological landscape of the United States. Their motivations, strategies, and the networks they build reveal a complex web of power that extends far beyond traditional party lines or public debate.

As the story unfolds, the intricate connections between money, ideology, and policy become increasingly apparent. The narrative traces how these financial titans—often with deeply personal convictions—have shaped the rise of the radical right, influencing everything from climate policy to judicial appointments. Through a detailed exploration of their methods and alliances, the far-reaching consequences of concentrated wealth on democracy and governance are brought into sharp focus, inviting a deeper reflection on the true nature of political power in modern America.
