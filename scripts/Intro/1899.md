## Introduction

Disappearance leaves a unique kind of ache—an absence that is never quite filled, a question that never finds its answer. In Vanished: True Stories from Families of Australian Missing Persons by Nicole Morris, the emotional landscape of those left behind comes into sharp focus. The stories unfold through the eyes of families who live with the daily uncertainty of not knowing what happened to their loved ones. Each account reveals the ripple effects of loss, hope, and the relentless search for closure, painting a portrait of resilience in the face of the unknown.

The journey through these true stories is both intimate and wide-reaching, capturing the diversity of experiences across Australia. From the first moments of realization to the long, drawn-out years of waiting, the narratives highlight the strength and vulnerability of families navigating a world forever changed. The collective voices echo with longing, determination, and the enduring hope that answers may still be found. Through these lived experiences, the complexities of grief, community support, and the ongoing impact of unresolved disappearances are brought to light, inviting a deeper understanding of what it means to live with uncertainty.
