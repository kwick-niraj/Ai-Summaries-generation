## Introduction

Patterns of self-sabotage often weave quietly through daily life, shaping choices and emotions in ways that feel both familiar and frustrating. The world of How to Stop Feeling Like Sh*t by Andrea Owen explores the subtle, persistent habits that undermine confidence and well-being—habits like perfectionism, people-pleasing, and negative self-talk. These behaviors, while often adopted as coping mechanisms, can become barriers to genuine happiness and self-acceptance, leaving a sense of being stuck or overwhelmed by one’s own inner critic.

Within this landscape, the journey unfolds toward recognizing and dismantling these ingrained patterns. Compassion and honesty become essential tools, inviting a deeper understanding of why these habits take root and how they can be transformed. The process is not about quick fixes, but about cultivating awareness, resilience, and a willingness to embrace discomfort as a path to growth. As the layers of self-sabotage are peeled back, space emerges for authenticity, self-respect, and a more empowered way of living, setting the stage for meaningful change.
