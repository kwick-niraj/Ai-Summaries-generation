## Introduction

The Korean War stands as one of the pivotal conflicts of the twentieth century, shaping the destinies of nations and the lives of millions. Tensions simmered on the Korean Peninsula as ideological divides between North and South Korea, fueled by the broader Cold War rivalry, erupted into open warfare in 1950. The struggle quickly drew in global powers, with the United States and its allies supporting the South, while China and the Soviet Union backed the North. The war’s brutality, shifting frontlines, and devastating human cost left indelible marks on both the land and its people, echoing far beyond the armistice that eventually brought an uneasy ceasefire.

Korean War: A History From Beginning to End by Hourly History traces the origins of the conflict, the major battles and turning points, and the complex web of international interests that shaped its course. The narrative follows the rise of tensions after World War II, the dramatic invasion across the 38th parallel, and the relentless push and pull of armies across the peninsula. Through the lens of political intrigue, military strategy, and personal sacrifice, the story unfolds to reveal not only the immediate consequences of the war but also its lasting impact on global politics and the enduring division of Korea. The journey through these events offers a deeper understanding of a war often overshadowed, yet crucial to the modern world.
