## Introduction

History often arrives wrapped in the solemnity of textbooks and the authority of classroom lectures, yet beneath the surface lies a world brimming with untold stories, irreverent truths, and the kind of mischief that rarely makes it into official accounts. Shit my History Teacher DID NOT tell me! by Karl Wiggins plunges into the overlooked, the bizarre, and the downright hilarious corners of the past, challenging the sanitized versions of events that so many have come to accept. Here, the past is not a distant, dusty relic but a living, breathing spectacle—full of eccentric personalities, unexpected twists, and the kind of human folly that makes history feel startlingly alive.

With a sharp wit and a keen eye for the absurd, the journey through these unconventional tales reveals how much of what is accepted as fact is shaped by omission, bias, or sheer prudery. The stories that emerge are not just entertaining; they invite a deeper curiosity about the motivations, mistakes, and misadventures that have shaped the world. As the familiar timeline is upended, a new appreciation grows for the complexity and humor that history holds, setting the stage for a fresh perspective on the events and characters that have been hiding in plain sight.
