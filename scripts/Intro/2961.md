## Introduction

When familiar routines and long-held beliefs no longer provide comfort or direction, a sense of uncertainty can settle in, prompting a search for new meaning and ways of being. The journey through change often brings both vulnerability and hope, as old patterns fall away and the unknown stretches ahead. In I Guess I Haven't Learned That Yet: Discovering New Ways of Living When the Old Ways Stop Working, Shauna Jeffs explores the transformative power of embracing not-knowing, inviting a gentle curiosity toward life’s unanswered questions and unresolved challenges.

Navigating seasons of transition, the narrative unfolds with honesty and compassion, revealing how letting go of certainty can open doors to growth, resilience, and unexpected joy. Through personal stories and thoughtful reflection, the process of unlearning and relearning becomes a path toward authenticity and deeper connection—with oneself and with others. The invitation is to approach life with openness, to find courage in the midst of change, and to discover that sometimes, not having all the answers is the beginning of something new.
