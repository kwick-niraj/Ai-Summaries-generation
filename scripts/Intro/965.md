## Introduction

A vibrant world pulses with color, energy, and the restless curiosity of a young mind determined to make its mark. In Radiant Child: The Story of Young Artist Jean-Michel Basquiat by Javaka Steptoe, the journey of a boy who sees art everywhere—on city walls, in the rhythm of jazz, and in the stories of his Caribbean heritage—unfolds with raw emotion and boundless imagination. The city’s chaos and beauty become a canvas for dreams, while the encouragement of a loving mother nurtures a spirit that refuses to be contained by convention or expectation. The struggle to find a place in a world that often misunderstands difference is met with resilience, as creativity becomes both shield and sword.

Through the lens of Basquiat’s early life, the narrative explores the power of self-expression and the courage it takes to remain true to one’s vision. The challenges of growing up, the pain of loss, and the triumph of finding one’s voice are woven together in a tapestry that celebrates individuality and the transformative force of art. Each moment is infused with the hope that beauty can be found in the unexpected, and that a child’s radiant spirit can illuminate even the darkest corners. The story moves seamlessly from the intimate to the universal, inviting reflection on the ways creativity shapes identity and leaves a lasting legacy.
