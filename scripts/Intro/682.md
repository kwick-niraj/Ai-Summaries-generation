## Introduction

Romantic relationships often feel like a puzzle, with emotions, expectations, and communication styles fitting together in ways that can either nurture or undermine intimacy. The world of Attached: The New Science of Adult Attachment and How It Can Help You Find—and Keep—Love by Amir Levine delves into the powerful influence of attachment theory on adult relationships, revealing how early emotional bonds shape the way people connect, love, and respond to their partners. Patterns of attachment—secure, anxious, and avoidant—emerge as invisible forces, guiding the dance of closeness and distance, trust and vulnerability, that defines every partnership.

Understanding these patterns opens a window into the hidden dynamics that drive attraction, conflict, and satisfaction in love. By exploring the science behind attachment, the narrative uncovers why some individuals thrive in relationships while others struggle with insecurity or withdrawal. Practical insights illuminate how recognizing one’s own attachment style, as well as that of a partner, can transform the search for lasting connection. With this knowledge, the path to deeper intimacy and healthier bonds becomes clearer, inviting a new perspective on what it means to find—and keep—love.
