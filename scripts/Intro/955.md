## Introduction

Power, influence, and the pursuit of security have shaped the modern world, but beneath the surface lies a deeper struggle over the direction of global order. Hegemony or Survival: America's Quest for Global Dominance by Noam Chomsky delves into the forces that drive U.S. foreign policy, questioning the motives and consequences of a superpower’s relentless reach. The tension between national interests and the broader needs of humanity emerges as a central concern, raising urgent questions about the sustainability of a world shaped by unilateral decisions and military might. The specter of unchecked dominance looms large, challenging the very foundations of international cooperation and peace.

As the narrative unfolds, the intricate web of political, economic, and military strategies comes into focus, revealing patterns that have defined decades of international relations. The interplay between official rhetoric and covert actions exposes the complexities of global leadership, while the voices of dissent and resistance highlight the ongoing struggle for justice and self-determination. Through a critical lens, the dynamics of power are laid bare, inviting reflection on the paths that lead either toward lasting security or the peril of perpetual conflict. The stage is set for a deeper exploration of the choices that will determine the future of nations and the fate of the planet.
