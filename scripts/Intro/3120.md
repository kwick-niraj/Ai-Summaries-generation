## Introduction

Questions of belief, hope, and the search for meaning often arise in the quiet moments of life, especially when confronted with the stories of others who have walked very different paths. In Have a Little Faith: a True Story, Mitch Albom brings together the lives of two men—one a revered rabbi nearing the end of his days, the other a former convict turned pastor—each wrestling with faith in their own way. Through their journeys, the nature of trust, forgiveness, and the enduring power of community come into focus, revealing how faith can be both a comfort and a challenge, a source of strength and a call to action.

As the seasons change and life’s uncertainties unfold, the intertwining stories of these men illuminate the universal longing for connection and understanding. The wisdom of age, the struggles of redemption, and the small acts of kindness that ripple through communities all become threads in a tapestry that asks what it truly means to believe. The world of Have a Little Faith gently invites reflection on the ways faith shapes lives, not just in grand gestures, but in the everyday choices that define character and legacy, setting the stage for a deeper exploration of hope and humanity.
