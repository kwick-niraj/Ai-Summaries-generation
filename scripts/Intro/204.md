## Introduction

Instinct often feels like a trusted guide, especially in high-stakes business environments where quick decisions are celebrated. Yet, beneath the surface, cognitive biases and hidden mental shortcuts can quietly sabotage even the most experienced leaders. Never Go With Your Gut by Gleb Tsipursky delves into the fascinating world of decision-making, challenging the age-old wisdom that intuition is the best compass. Instead, it uncovers the psychological traps that lead to costly errors and explores how evidence-based strategies can transform the way choices are made at every level of leadership.

Within this landscape, the journey moves from the allure of gut feelings to the science of rational thinking, revealing how pioneering leaders consistently outperform their peers by questioning assumptions and embracing structured approaches. The narrative weaves together real-world examples, psychological research, and practical frameworks, illustrating how to recognize and counteract the subtle forces that distort judgment. As the exploration unfolds, the focus shifts toward actionable tools and mindsets that empower individuals and organizations to sidestep disaster and achieve lasting success.
