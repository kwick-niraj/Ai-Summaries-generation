## Introduction

The act of reading holds a unique place in shaping minds, nurturing faith, and cultivating wisdom. Within the Christian tradition, the written word has always been a powerful tool for spiritual growth and discernment, inviting believers to engage deeply with both sacred texts and the broader world of literature. Lit!: A Christian Guide to Reading Books by Tony Reinke explores the intersection of faith and reading, delving into how books can serve as companions on the journey of discipleship, intellectual development, and personal transformation. The questions of what to read, how to read, and why reading matters are woven together with the conviction that every page turned can be an act of worship and a means of encountering truth.

Navigating the vast landscape of literature, the guidance offered here encourages thoughtful engagement with both Christian and secular works, emphasizing discernment, humility, and joy in the reading process. Practical strategies blend with theological reflection, offering a vision of reading that is both purposeful and pleasurable. The invitation is to see books not merely as sources of information, but as instruments for shaping character, deepening understanding, and drawing closer to God. With this perspective, the world of reading opens up as a rich field for growth, connection, and lifelong learning, seamlessly leading into a deeper exploration of how to read well and wisely.
