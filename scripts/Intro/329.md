## Introduction

Behind the enigmatic borders of North Korea, daily life unfolds in ways that defy the world’s expectations. The rhythms of commerce, ambition, and adaptation pulse quietly beneath the surface of a society often shrouded in secrecy. In A Capitalist in North Korea: My Seven Years in the Hermit Kingdom by Felix Abt, the unlikely intersection of entrepreneurship and authoritarianism comes to life, revealing how business and personal relationships are forged in a place where ideology and survival intermingle. The narrative explores the challenges and small victories of building enterprises in a land where every transaction is colored by politics, suspicion, and the ever-present gaze of the state.

Through the lens of a foreign businessman navigating the complexities of North Korean society, the story uncovers the resourcefulness and resilience of individuals striving for progress within rigid constraints. Everyday moments—negotiating with officials, training local staff, and adapting to cultural nuances—illuminate the subtle ways in which hope and pragmatism coexist. The journey is marked by moments of connection, misunderstanding, and unexpected camaraderie, offering a rare glimpse into the human side of a country often defined by its isolation. As the world outside speculates and judges, life inside North Korea continues, shaped by the quiet determination of those who dare to dream, even in the most unlikely of places.
