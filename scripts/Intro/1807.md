## Introduction

In a world often divided by race and circumstance, the story of Covert, Michigan, emerges as a testament to the possibilities of unity and shared purpose. A Stronger Kinship by Anna-Lisa Cox delves into the remarkable journey of a small town that, against the backdrop of post-Civil War America, chose inclusion over division. Here, Black and white families built lives side by side, forging bonds that defied the era’s prevailing prejudices. The town’s commitment to equality and mutual respect stands as a beacon of hope, challenging the notion that segregation and animosity were inevitable outcomes of the time.

Through the lens of Covert’s history, the narrative unfolds with the struggles and triumphs of its residents, revealing how faith, resilience, and a shared vision can transform a community. The intertwining lives of its people—farmers, teachers, preachers, and children—paint a vivid portrait of what it means to create a society rooted in justice and compassion. As the story moves through decades of change, the enduring spirit of kinship and the courage to stand together in the face of adversity offer a powerful reminder of the impact that ordinary individuals can have when guided by hope and faith.
