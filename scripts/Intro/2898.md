## Introduction

Invisible forces often shape the way people perceive and interact with one another, quietly influencing decisions, judgments, and the quality of relationships. The Blindspots Between Us: How to Overcome Unconscious Cognitive Bias and Build Better Relationships by Gleb Tsipursky delves into the subtle yet powerful impact of cognitive biases on everyday connections. These mental shortcuts, while designed to help navigate a complex world, can create misunderstandings, fuel conflict, and prevent genuine empathy from flourishing. Recognizing and addressing these hidden patterns becomes essential for anyone seeking to foster trust, understanding, and authentic communication.

Within this landscape, the journey unfolds toward greater self-awareness and intentionality in relationships. By exploring the roots of unconscious bias and offering practical strategies for overcoming them, the path opens to more meaningful interactions—whether with family, friends, colleagues, or strangers. The process of uncovering these blindspots not only transforms individual perspectives but also paves the way for stronger, more resilient bonds. As the exploration deepens, the promise emerges: relationships can thrive when the unseen barriers of bias are brought into the light and compassion takes the lead.
