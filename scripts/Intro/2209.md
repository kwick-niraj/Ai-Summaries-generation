## Introduction

Stepping into leadership often means navigating a landscape filled with both opportunity and challenge, especially for women forging their path in business and the workplace. The journey calls for a blend of confidence, strategic thinking, and emotional intelligence, as well as the ability to inspire and empower others. She Thinks Like a Boss: Leadership—9 Essential Skills for New Female Leaders in Business and the Workplace by Jemma Roedel delves into the unique dynamics women encounter as they rise to positions of influence, highlighting the importance of self-awareness, resilience, and effective communication. The world of leadership is not just about authority, but about cultivating a mindset that embraces growth, collaboration, and the courage to break through barriers.

Within this framework, the focus turns to the practical and transformative skills that can elevate new female leaders. From mastering assertiveness and decision-making to building trust and fostering inclusive teams, each essential skill becomes a stepping stone toward authentic leadership. The narrative weaves together actionable insights and empowering perspectives, encouraging women to claim their space and lead with purpose. As the journey unfolds, the emphasis remains on developing a leadership style that is both impactful and true to oneself, setting the stage for lasting success and meaningful change.
