## Introduction

Innocence and justice often collide in unexpected ways, revealing the fragile boundaries between truth and error. The world of Framed: Astonishing True Stories of Wrongful Convictions by John Grisham plunges into the unsettling reality where ordinary lives are upended by the machinery of the legal system. Through a tapestry of real-life cases, the narrative exposes the human cost of mistakes, misjudgments, and systemic flaws that can lead to devastating consequences for those wrongly accused. The emotional weight of these stories underscores the profound impact of a single error, not only on individuals but on families and communities, challenging the very foundation of trust in the pursuit of justice.

As each case unfolds, the intricate interplay of evidence, circumstance, and human fallibility comes to light, painting a vivid portrait of the complexities within the criminal justice system. The relentless search for truth, the resilience of those fighting for exoneration, and the tireless efforts of advocates all converge to highlight both the darkness and hope that exist within these stories. The journey through these astonishing accounts invites reflection on the meaning of justice, the importance of vigilance, and the enduring strength of the human spirit in the face of overwhelming odds.
