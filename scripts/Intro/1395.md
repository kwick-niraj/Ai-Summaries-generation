## Introduction

Imagination, storytelling, and the enduring power of words come alive in the world of Neil Gaiman, where boundaries between reality and fantasy blur and the ordinary becomes extraordinary. The View from the Cheap Seats: Selected Nonfiction invites reflection on the role of stories in shaping lives, the importance of art and creativity, and the ways in which culture, myth, and memory intertwine. Through essays, speeches, and musings, the landscape of literature, comics, music, and personal experience unfolds, revealing how stories serve as both mirrors and windows—reflecting inner truths and offering glimpses into other worlds.

Wandering through libraries, comic conventions, and the quiet corners of everyday life, a tapestry of insights emerges, woven from encounters with beloved books, cherished authors, and the creative process itself. The journey is one of curiosity and reverence, where the act of reading and writing becomes a celebration of human connection and the magic found in the mundane. With warmth and wit, Neil Gaiman’s perspective encourages a deeper appreciation for the stories that shape identity and community, gently guiding toward a richer understanding of why stories matter and how they linger long after the final page is turned.
