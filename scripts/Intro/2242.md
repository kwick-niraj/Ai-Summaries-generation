## Introduction

Ambition and talent often propel careers forward, yet subtle habits and ingrained behaviors can quietly undermine progress, especially for women navigating the complexities of professional advancement. In How Women Rise: Break the 12 Habits Holding You Back from Your Next Raise, Promotion, or Job by Sally Helgesen, the spotlight turns to the unique challenges women face in the workplace—challenges that are not always about skill or effort, but about patterns of thinking and acting that can inadvertently stall growth. The journey toward leadership and recognition is shaped not only by external barriers but also by internalized habits that may have once served a purpose but now stand in the way of new opportunities.

Within this landscape, the focus shifts to twelve specific habits that often hold women back, from reluctance to claim achievements to the tendency to overvalue expertise at the expense of visibility. Each habit is explored with empathy and insight, revealing how even the most well-intentioned behaviors can become obstacles to advancement. The path to breaking these patterns is not about wholesale change, but about recognizing strengths, reframing self-perceptions, and embracing new strategies for influence and growth. As the narrative unfolds, the possibility of transformation emerges—not just for individual careers, but for the broader culture of work itself.
