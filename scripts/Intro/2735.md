## Introduction

From the first sip of ancient beer to the clink of a modern cocktail, the evolution of civilization has been intimately tied to the drinks that have quenched humanity’s thirst. A History of the World in 6 Glasses by Tom Standage traces the remarkable journey of six iconic beverages—beer, wine, spirits, coffee, tea, and cola—revealing how each has shaped societies, economies, and cultures across continents and centuries. These drinks are more than mere refreshments; they are vessels of power, symbols of social change, and catalysts for innovation, each leaving an indelible mark on the course of human history.

As the story unfolds, the rise and fall of empires, the spread of ideas, and the forging of global connections are all seen through the lens of what people drank and why. Beer fosters the earliest agricultural communities, wine becomes the lifeblood of classical antiquity, and spirits fuel exploration and commerce. Coffee energizes intellectual revolutions, tea underpins imperial ambitions, and cola emerges as a symbol of modernity and globalization. Through these six glasses, the interconnectedness of human progress and daily ritual comes alive, inviting a deeper appreciation for the ordinary drinks that have shaped extraordinary destinies.
