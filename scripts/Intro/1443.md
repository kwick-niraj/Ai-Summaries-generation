## Introduction

A sense of sanctuary and well-being often begins at home, where the rhythms of daily life are shaped by the care and attention given to one’s surroundings. The subtle interplay between order, cleanliness, and comfort forms the foundation of a nurturing environment, inviting both relaxation and productivity. In Home Comforts: The Art and Science of Keeping House, Cheryl Mendelson delves into the intricate balance between tradition and practicality, exploring how the smallest domestic rituals can transform a living space into a true haven. The act of keeping house emerges not as a mundane chore, but as a meaningful practice that fosters health, happiness, and a sense of belonging.

From the science behind dust and laundry to the art of setting a welcoming table, every aspect of domestic life is illuminated with both warmth and precision. The journey through home care reveals the hidden logic behind routines, the satisfaction of well-kept spaces, and the quiet joy found in everyday tasks. Practical wisdom is woven together with cultural insights, offering a holistic perspective on what it means to create comfort and order. As the exploration unfolds, the value of mindful housekeeping becomes clear, inviting a deeper appreciation for the spaces that shelter and sustain.
