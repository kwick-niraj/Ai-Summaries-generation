## Introduction

Money, power, and gender intersect in ways that shape not only individual lives but the very fabric of society. The world of Financial Feminist: Overcome the Patriarchy's Bullsh*t to Master Your Money and Build a Life You Love by Tori Dunlap confronts the deeply rooted systems that have historically kept women from achieving financial independence and security. The narrative challenges the status quo, questioning why financial advice so often ignores the unique barriers women face and how reclaiming financial agency can become a radical act of self-empowerment. At its heart, the journey is about more than just dollars and cents—it’s about rewriting the rules, breaking free from limiting beliefs, and building a life defined by choice rather than circumstance.

Through a blend of practical strategies and bold, unapologetic encouragement, the path unfolds toward mastering money on one’s own terms. The exploration moves from understanding the cultural and systemic forces at play to actionable steps for budgeting, investing, negotiating, and building wealth. Along the way, stories and insights illuminate the emotional side of money, the importance of community, and the power of collective change. The invitation is clear: step into a new relationship with money, one that is rooted in confidence, clarity, and a vision for a life truly loved.
