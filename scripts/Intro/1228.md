## Introduction

Aging often arrives with a host of expectations—some hopeful, many daunting. Yet, beneath the surface of each passing year lies the potential for renewal, curiosity, and even joy. Rejuvenaging: The Art and Science of Growing Older with Enthusiasm by Bill Gifford invites a fresh perspective on the later chapters of life, challenging the notion that aging must be synonymous with decline. Instead, the journey through time becomes an opportunity to harness both scientific insight and personal passion, revealing how vitality and enthusiasm can flourish well into the golden years.

Within this landscape, the interplay between cutting-edge research and the wisdom of lived experience takes center stage. The pursuit of longevity is not just about adding years, but about enriching them—cultivating habits, mindsets, and relationships that foster resilience and zest. The narrative weaves together practical strategies, inspiring stories, and the latest discoveries in health and wellness, all pointing toward a life where age becomes a source of strength rather than limitation. The path forward is illuminated by the promise that growing older can be a process of continual rejuvenation, setting the stage for a deeper exploration of how to thrive at every stage.
