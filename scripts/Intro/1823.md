## Introduction

Conventional wisdom often paints capitalism as a flawless engine of prosperity, innovation, and freedom, but beneath the surface, contradictions and hidden truths shape its real-world impact. In 23 Things They Don't Tell You About Capitalism, Ha-Joon Chang challenges the myths and assumptions that underpin modern economic thinking, inviting a closer look at the forces that drive inequality, instability, and unexpected outcomes. The familiar narratives of free markets and self-regulation are questioned, revealing how policies and ideologies, rather than natural laws, determine who benefits and who is left behind.

Exploring the intricate web of global finance, labor, and government intervention, the discussion moves beyond simplistic slogans to uncover the complex realities of how economies function. Each "thing" exposes a different facet of capitalism, from the myth of meritocracy to the overlooked role of state support in fostering innovation. The journey through these insights reveals a world where economic success is shaped as much by collective choices and historical context as by individual effort. With each revelation, the path opens toward a more nuanced understanding of prosperity, fairness, and the possibilities for change.
