## Introduction

Ancient monuments, enigmatic myths, and unexplained archaeological discoveries hint at a forgotten chapter in human history, one that challenges the conventional timeline of civilization. Fingerprints of the Gods: The Evidence of Earth's Lost Civilization by Graham Hancock delves into the mysteries that surround humanity’s distant past, weaving together clues from across the globe. The possibility of a highly advanced civilization, lost to cataclysm and time, emerges through patterns in architecture, astronomy, and ancient lore—inviting a reexamination of what is truly known about the origins of culture and knowledge.

As the journey unfolds, connections between the pyramids of Egypt, the ruins of South America, and the myths of flood and rebirth begin to form a tapestry of evidence. The search for answers leads through scientific puzzles, ancient maps, and the enduring question of how such sophisticated knowledge could have existed millennia before recorded history. The narrative invites a sense of wonder and curiosity, opening the door to the idea that humanity’s story may be far older and more complex than previously imagined.
