## Introduction

A century of living offers a rare vantage point on the world, where the ordinary and extraordinary blend into a tapestry of wisdom, resilience, and joy. In Wake Up With Purpose!: What I’ve Learned in My First Hundred Years, Jean Dolores Schmidt—affectionately known as Sister Jean—embodies the spirit of optimism and faith that has carried her through decades of change, challenge, and triumph. Her journey is marked by a steadfast commitment to service, a deep-rooted sense of community, and an unwavering belief in the power of kindness and perseverance. Through the lens of her remarkable life, the enduring values of hope, gratitude, and purposeful living come alive, inviting reflection on what truly matters.

From the bustling halls of schools to the electrifying energy of basketball courts, Sister Jean’s story unfolds as a celebration of connection, mentorship, and the simple joys that give each day meaning. Her reflections reveal the lessons gleaned from a lifetime of teaching, coaching, and inspiring others, offering gentle guidance on navigating adversity with grace and humor. The narrative weaves together moments of personal growth, faith, and the transformative impact of caring for others, creating a mosaic of insights that resonate across generations. As the journey continues, the invitation emerges to embrace each morning with intention and to find purpose in every season of life.
