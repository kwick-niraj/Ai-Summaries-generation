## Introduction

Sacrifice, love, and redemption intertwine in the story of the cross, where every detail carries profound meaning. The journey through Christ’s final hours reveals not only the pain endured but also the depth of divine affection that seeks to reach every heart. In He Chose the Nails: What God Did to Win Your Heart by Max Lucado, the focus turns to the symbols and moments surrounding the crucifixion—each nail, each crown of thorns, each gesture—unveiling the lengths to which God goes to demonstrate unwavering love and grace.

As the narrative unfolds, the significance of Christ’s choices becomes clear, inviting reflection on the personal nature of this sacrifice. The cross is not merely a historical event but a living testament to the relentless pursuit of connection and forgiveness. Through vivid imagery and heartfelt exploration, the story draws out the hope, mercy, and transformation offered to all who encounter the message of the cross, setting the stage for a deeper understanding of faith and belonging.
