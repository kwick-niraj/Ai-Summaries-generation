## Introduction

A world shrouded in secrecy and fear, North Korea stands as one of the most enigmatic and tightly controlled societies on earth. Within its borders, daily life is shaped by a relentless cult of personality, pervasive surveillance, and the ever-present threat of punishment for even the smallest perceived disloyalty. The regime’s grip extends into the minds and hearts of its people, demanding unwavering devotion while sowing seeds of suspicion and isolation. In this landscape, the boundaries between truth and propaganda blur, and the cost of independent thought can be devastatingly high.

Dear Leader: Poet, Spy, Escapee - A Look Inside North Korea by Jang Jin-sung draws from the rare vantage point of an insider who once occupied a privileged position within the regime’s inner circles. Through the eyes of a favored poet-turned-defector, the realities of power, fear, and survival come into sharp focus. The journey from trusted propagandist to hunted fugitive reveals not only the mechanisms of control but also the resilience of the human spirit in the face of oppression. As the layers of secrecy are peeled away, the stark truths of life under dictatorship emerge, offering a rare glimpse into both the machinery of the regime and the quiet acts of resistance that persist within its shadow.
