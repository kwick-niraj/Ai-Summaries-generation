## Introduction

A single gunshot in Sarajevo set the world on a path to unprecedented conflict, forever altering the course of history. The Trigger: Hunting the Assassin Who Brought the World to War by Tim Butcher delves into the tangled web of fate, ideology, and circumstance that led Gavrilo Princip to assassinate Archduke Franz Ferdinand in 1914. The story unfolds against the backdrop of a fractured Europe, where nationalism, imperial ambition, and youthful idealism collided with devastating consequences. Through the lens of Princip’s life and the turbulent Balkans, the narrative explores how personal conviction and historical forces can converge to ignite global upheaval.

Winding through the landscapes and memories of the Balkans, the journey retraces Princip’s footsteps, seeking to understand the motivations and environment that shaped him. The search for meaning in a single act of violence becomes a meditation on the nature of destiny, the power of place, and the enduring scars of war. As the past echoes through the present, the story reveals how the legacy of one young man’s actions continues to haunt and inform the world, inviting reflection on the fragile threads that bind history together.
