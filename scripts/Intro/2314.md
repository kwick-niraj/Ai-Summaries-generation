## Introduction

Attention acts as both a spotlight and a filter, shaping reality by illuminating certain details while leaving others in shadow. In the world of Goodpoint by Roberto F. Canducci, the mind’s tendency to focus on specific elements often determines not just perception, but the very course of life itself. The interplay between what is noticed and what is overlooked becomes a powerful force, quietly influencing decisions, relationships, and opportunities. The unseen, though hidden from conscious awareness, holds the potential to alter destinies, reminding that the boundaries of experience are often self-imposed by the limits of focus.

Navigating this landscape reveals how selective attention can both empower and constrain. The journey unfolds through the subtle art of shifting perspective, inviting a deeper awareness of the unnoticed and the transformative impact it can have. By exploring the mechanisms of focus and the consequences of neglecting the periphery, a richer, more nuanced understanding of life emerges. The invitation is to look beyond the obvious, to recognize the silent forces at play, and to embrace the possibility that what lies outside the current field of vision may hold the key to profound change.
