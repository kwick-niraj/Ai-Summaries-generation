## Introduction

Pregnancy unfolds as a journey filled with anticipation, questions, and profound transformation. The experience brings together a swirl of emotions—excitement, anxiety, curiosity, and hope—while the body and mind adapt to the promise of new life. Navigating this period means encountering a cascade of physical changes, emotional shifts, and practical decisions, all of which can feel both exhilarating and overwhelming. The world of What to Expect When You're Expecting by Heidi Murkoff immerses itself in the realities of pregnancy, offering a compassionate lens on the milestones, challenges, and joys that define this unique chapter.

From the earliest signs and symptoms to the final days before birth, every stage is marked by its own set of wonders and worries. Guidance emerges on everything from nutrition and prenatal care to relationships, work, and preparing for labor. The evolving needs of both parent and baby are explored with empathy, demystifying common concerns and celebrating the small victories along the way. As the journey progresses, reassurance and practical wisdom become steady companions, gently illuminating the path toward parenthood and easing the uncertainties that naturally arise.
