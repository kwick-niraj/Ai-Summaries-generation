## Introduction

Towering walls of water, both feared and revered, have shaped human history and imagination for centuries. The Wave by Susan Casey plunges into the heart of these oceanic giants, exploring the science, mystery, and raw power behind the world’s most massive waves. From the relentless pursuit of big-wave surfers to the meticulous calculations of scientists, the narrative weaves together stories of adventure, risk, and discovery. The ocean’s unpredictable temperament becomes a stage for human ambition and awe, where the boundaries between mastery and humility are constantly tested.

As the journey unfolds, the relentless energy of the sea is matched by the determination of those who seek to understand or conquer it. The lives of mariners, researchers, and thrill-seekers intersect in a world where nature’s extremes reveal both beauty and danger. Through vivid encounters with rogue waves and the people who chase them, the exploration moves from the depths of scientific inquiry to the edge of human endurance. The pulse of the ocean, ever-changing and untamable, invites a deeper reflection on the relationship between humanity and the natural world, setting the stage for a gripping exploration of risk, resilience, and wonder.
