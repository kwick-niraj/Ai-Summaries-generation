## Introduction

The intricate dance between sound and existence unfolds in Metaphysics of Sound by Nataša Pantović, where the vibrations that shape the universe are explored not just as physical phenomena, but as profound carriers of meaning and consciousness. Sound emerges as a bridge between the tangible and the intangible, inviting reflection on how frequencies, resonance, and silence influence the very fabric of reality. The interplay of music, language, and spiritual tradition reveals a world where every note and every word holds the potential to transform perception and awaken deeper understanding.

Within this exploration, ancient wisdom and modern inquiry converge, tracing the roots of sound through myth, science, and mysticism. The journey moves from the origins of creation myths to the subtle harmonics that govern both nature and the human mind, suggesting that sound is not merely heard, but experienced as a force that shapes identity, memory, and connection. As the layers of meaning unfold, the resonance of sound becomes a guide toward greater self-awareness and a more profound relationship with the world, setting the stage for a transformative encounter with the unseen dimensions of life.
