## Introduction

Salt, a mineral so common today that it is often taken for granted, has shaped civilizations, driven economies, and sparked wars throughout human history. The story of salt is one of ingenuity and survival, where entire empires rose and fell on the control of this precious resource. From ancient trade routes that connected distant cultures to the birth of cities and the development of preservation techniques, salt’s influence reaches deep into the fabric of society. Its role in religious rituals, its symbolism in language, and its impact on the world’s cuisines reveal a substance that is far more than a simple seasoning.

Mark Kurlansky’s exploration of salt uncovers the intricate ways in which this mineral has been intertwined with the progress of humanity. The journey moves from the salt mines of ancient China to the salt roads of Africa, from the salt taxes that fueled revolutions to the scientific breakthroughs that changed how salt is produced and consumed. Along the way, the narrative weaves together stories of innovation, conflict, and adaptation, showing how salt has been a catalyst for change and a silent witness to the unfolding of history. The enduring legacy of salt offers a lens through which to view the interconnectedness of culture, technology, and power, inviting a deeper appreciation for the ordinary elements that shape the world.
