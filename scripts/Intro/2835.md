## Introduction

A life shaped by the wilds of East Africa unfolds against a backdrop of untamed landscapes and boundless skies. Adventure, resilience, and the pursuit of freedom pulse through every moment, as the world of early twentieth-century Kenya comes alive with the rhythms of horses’ hooves and the roar of primitive aircraft engines. The journey is not just across continents, but into the heart of what it means to live boldly, to embrace risk, and to find one’s place in a world that is both beautiful and unforgiving. In West with the Night by Beryl Markham, the spirit of exploration and the call of the unknown are ever-present, inviting a deeper understanding of courage and self-reliance.

From the first lessons learned as a horse trainer to the daring flights across vast, uncharted territories, each experience is woven with a sense of wonder and determination. Encounters with both the land and its people reveal the complexities of colonial Africa, while the skies offer a new frontier for ambition and discovery. The narrative moves seamlessly between earth and air, capturing the exhilaration of flight and the quiet moments of reflection that come with solitude. Through these intertwined adventures, the boundaries between past and present, fear and triumph, blur, setting the stage for a story that is as much about the inner journey as the physical one.
