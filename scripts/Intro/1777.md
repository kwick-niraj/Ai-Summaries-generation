## Introduction

Pain and suffering often feel like inescapable parts of the human experience, shaping daily life and coloring every interaction. The search for relief can lead down many paths, yet true healing remains elusive for so many. What Healing Should Be: How to relieve pain and stop suffering by George Alexandru delves into the heart of what it means to heal, challenging conventional notions and inviting a deeper exploration of the mind-body connection. The journey toward relief is not just about alleviating symptoms, but about understanding the roots of suffering and embracing a holistic approach that honors both physical and emotional well-being.

Within this perspective, healing emerges as a dynamic process—one that requires self-awareness, compassion, and a willingness to confront the underlying causes of pain. George Alexandru weaves together insights from science, philosophy, and personal experience, illuminating the pathways that lead to genuine transformation. The exploration moves beyond quick fixes, encouraging a shift in mindset that empowers individuals to reclaim agency over their health. As the layers of suffering are gently peeled away, a new vision of healing takes shape—one that is accessible, sustainable, and deeply human, setting the stage for a profound shift in how pain and relief are understood.
