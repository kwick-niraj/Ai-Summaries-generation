## Introduction

The accelerating rise of artificial intelligence is reshaping the very foundations of economic decision-making, challenging long-held assumptions about value, labor, and the nature of prediction itself. As AI systems become increasingly adept at forecasting outcomes, the boundaries between human judgment and machine-driven insight begin to blur, raising profound questions about who holds power in a world where prediction is no longer a uniquely human domain. The interplay between technological innovation and economic disruption takes center stage, inviting a closer look at how organizations, markets, and societies adapt—or fail to adapt—to the shifting landscape of possibility and risk.

Power And Prediction: The Disruptive Economics of Artificial Intelligence by Ajay Agrawal delves into the transformative impact of AI on industries, institutions, and everyday life. The narrative traces the ripple effects of predictive technologies, exploring how they alter the cost structures of businesses, redefine competitive advantage, and create new winners and losers. Through the lens of economic theory and real-world examples, the story unfolds to reveal both the opportunities and the challenges that arise when machines take on the role of forecasters. The journey through these ideas sets the stage for a deeper understanding of the choices and consequences that define the age of artificial intelligence.
