## Introduction

In the fast-paced world of leadership, the art of asking the right questions often holds more transformative power than offering quick answers. The Coaching Habit: Say Less, Ask More & Change the Way You Lead Forever by Michael Bungay Stanier delves into the subtle yet profound shift from telling to asking, revealing how curiosity and thoughtful inquiry can unlock potential in teams and individuals alike. The focus moves away from traditional top-down management, instead embracing a culture where leaders empower others by listening deeply and guiding with intention. This approach not only fosters trust and engagement but also cultivates a space where growth and innovation thrive.

As the journey unfolds, practical frameworks and actionable strategies emerge, designed to seamlessly integrate coaching into everyday conversations. The emphasis lies on simplicity—seven essential questions become the backbone of meaningful dialogue, making coaching accessible and sustainable even amidst daily pressures. Through this lens, leadership transforms from a role of authority to one of partnership, where every interaction becomes an opportunity for development. The path forward is clear: by saying less and asking more, leaders can inspire lasting change and elevate the way they lead, one conversation at a time.
