## Introduction

Loss, hope, and the unexpected ways love can reshape a life come together in the world of Finding Chika: A Little Girl, an Earthquake, and the Making of a Family by Mitch Albom. The story unfolds in the aftermath of the devastating earthquake in Haiti, where the fragility of life is met with the resilience of the human spirit. At the heart of this journey is Chika, a spirited young girl whose presence challenges and transforms the boundaries of family, belonging, and what it means to care for another. Through the lens of her illness and the search for healing, the narrative explores the profound connections that can form between people from vastly different worlds, and the courage it takes to open one’s heart to both joy and sorrow.

As the journey progresses, the lines between caregiver and child, teacher and student, blur in unexpected ways. The experience of nurturing Chika through her illness becomes a mirror for examining the meaning of family, the depths of compassion, and the enduring impact of loss. Mitch Albom’s exploration of these themes invites reflection on the ways love can be both a source of pain and a wellspring of strength, offering a testament to the transformative power of human connection. The story’s emotional landscape gently leads into a deeper understanding of what it means to truly embrace another, even in the face of uncertainty.
