## Introduction

When the world was shaken by the events of September 11, 2001, a small town on the edge of North America became an unexpected haven for thousands of stranded airline passengers. The Day the World Came to Town: 9/11 in Gander, Newfoundland by Jim DeFede reveals how, in the midst of global chaos and fear, the people of Gander opened their homes and hearts to strangers from across the globe. Acts of kindness, generosity, and solidarity emerged as the town’s residents provided food, shelter, and comfort, transforming a moment of crisis into a testament to the power of community and compassion.

As the skies closed and uncertainty loomed, Gander’s population nearly doubled overnight, creating a unique tapestry of human connection. Stories of resilience, empathy, and unexpected friendships unfolded, highlighting the extraordinary capacity for goodness in ordinary people. Through the lens of this small Canadian town, the narrative explores how adversity can bring out the best in humanity, offering a powerful reminder that even in the darkest times, hope and unity can flourish. The journey into Gander’s response to 9/11 sets the stage for a deeper understanding of the enduring impact of kindness and collective action.
