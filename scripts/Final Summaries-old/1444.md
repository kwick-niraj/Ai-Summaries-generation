## 1. Discovering a Lost World in the Rocky Mountains

Sometimes, the most extraordinary stories begin in the most unassuming places. Imagine a summer day in the early 20th century, high in the Canadian Rockies, where the air is thin and the ground is littered with ancient stones. The site, later known as the Burgess Shale, quickly becomes a legend among paleontologists. The more the explorers look, the more they realize that the Burgess Shale is not just a window into the past—it’s a challenge to everything they thought they knew about the history of life.

As the years pass, the Burgess Shale becomes a symbol of both discovery and mystery. Some see the site as a treasure trove, others as a puzzle that may never be solved.

But beneath all the debate, one thing is clear: the Burgess Shale offers a rare glimpse into a world that vanished long ago, a world where life was far more diverse and unpredictable than anyone had imagined.
