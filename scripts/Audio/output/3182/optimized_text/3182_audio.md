Whether it’s owning up to a mistake, questioning an old belief, or simply being willing to say, “I don’t know”—that willingness to be honest and skeptical leads to better understanding and stronger connections.
 
 At the end of the day, science is a deeply human journey. Behind every discovery, there are people—people with doubts, hopes, and quirks.
