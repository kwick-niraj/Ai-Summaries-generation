 Here’s the thing: most people don’t even notice this kind of work, because it’s supposed to be invisible. The whole point is to keep things running smoothly, to stop problems before they even start. But the cost? It’s huge. Over time, the person doing all this extra work starts to feel invisible, too. Their efforts get taken for granted, and their own needs keep getting pushed aside.

Suddenly, you can have conversations you never could before.  Of course, just having a name for it doesn’t mean the problem disappears overnight. There’s still pushback, from others and even from ourselves.  Still, having the words gives you power. You start to notice when the balance is off, and it gets a little easier to speak up when something feels unfair. With time, small changes start to build. The work is still there, but it’s not invisible anymore—and that alone can make all the difference.
