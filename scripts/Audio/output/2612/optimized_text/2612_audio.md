The tools of death were simple: machetes, clubs, and sometimes guns. The killers were not faceless monsters but ordinary people—farmers, teachers, shopkeepers—driven by fear, hatred, or the simple pressure to conform.

Some tried to resist. A priest opened his church to those seeking refuge, believing that the sanctity of the place would protect them. For a time, it did. But eventually, the killers came, and the walls that had once offered safety became a trap. Survivors later spoke of the silence that followed, a silence filled with the weight of loss and betrayal.

Elsewhere, a young man hid in a neighbor’s attic, listening as the search parties passed below. He counted the footsteps, the shouts, the sudden silences. Each day was a test of endurance, a battle against hunger, fear, and the urge to give up. He survived, but the cost was more than he could have imagined.

The machinery of genocide was both organized and chaotic. Lists were drawn up, names checked off, and bodies disposed of with chilling efficiency. Yet, in the midst of the horror, there were moments of confusion and hesitation. Some refused to kill, risking their own lives to protect others. A few managed to smuggle friends to safety, hiding them in cellars or leading them through the hills under cover of darkness.

For most, though, the choice was stark: participate or perish. The pressure to conform was immense, and the consequences of refusal were often fatal. The violence fed on itself, growing more brutal as the days passed. The world, watching from a distance, struggled to comprehend the scale of the atrocity. News reports spoke of “tribal conflict” and “civil war,” missing the reality of a planned, systematic campaign to wipe out an entire people.

In the aftermath, survivors emerged from hiding, blinking in the harsh light of a world forever changed. The streets were littered with the evidence of what had happened—shattered homes, abandoned belongings, and the unspoken grief of those who remained. The question that lingered was simple but impossible to answer: how could this have happened?
