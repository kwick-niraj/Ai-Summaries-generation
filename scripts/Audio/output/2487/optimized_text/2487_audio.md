 Stories like these teach us something important: perception isn’t passive. Your brain is always interpreting and predicting—sometimes even inventing—what it thinks should be there. Sometimes, it bends reality just to make the pieces fit.  So what’s the big picture here? Reality isn’t just sitting out there, waiting for us to find it. It’s built, moment by moment, from your brain’s best guesses. The mind’s illusions aren’t failures—they’re the cost of a system built for speed, efficiency, and survival. When the machinery slips up, the results can be odd, even heartbreaking, but they also invite us to peek behind the curtain and see just how our minds really work.
