 Then we have **game mechanics**. Competition, achievements, or even just a little status boost can really drive sharing. Think about the apps that reward you with badges or points, or those loyalty programs at your favorite coffee shop. When people earn something, they want others to see it. Sharing those badges or showing off a high score isn’t just about the achievement—it’s about the recognition you get from other people.

If you’re one of them, you feel like an insider—and chances are, you’ll tell your friends. Pretty soon, word spreads. The restaurant gets more customers talking, all without spending a dime on extra advertising.
 
 Social currency goes way beyond just products or services. It’s everywhere—in the articles we share to look smart, the jokes we pass on to seem witty, or the causes we support to show our values. Underneath it all, there’s the same motivation: we want to look good in the eyes of others.
 
 So, how can you tap into this? It starts with designing things that people actually want to talk about. That means focusing on what makes something truly remarkable, giving people a taste of exclusivity, or building in ways for them to show off their achievements. And don’t forget to make sharing easy—whether it’s through social media, word of mouth, or even just a quick text.
 
 If you want to build social currency into your ideas or products, try these practical steps:
 
 Create features or experiences that surprise people or give them a great story. Offer exclusive access or insider info. And add some friendly competition or ways to recognize achievements.
 
 At the end of the day, when people feel that sharing something will make them look good, you’d better believe they’ll spread the word. And that’s how you make your ideas and products truly contagious.
