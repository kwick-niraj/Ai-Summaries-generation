Here, you do the task from memory, then pause to double-check the list and make sure nothing slipped through the cracks.  Which one should you use? Well, it depends. If you’re working in a fast-paced environment and know the process well, a do-confirm checklist might be just right. But if the task is more complicated or unfamiliar, a read-do approach gives you that extra bit of security.
 
 Timing is also key. The best checklists are built into natural pause points in your routine—those moments where it makes sense to stop and run through a quick review.
