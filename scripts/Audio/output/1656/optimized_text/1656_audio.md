These prophets understood that rituals and commandments alone couldn’t save anyone. Without the grace of the Redeemer, tradition is empty. Their testimonies cut through centuries of habit, reminding everyone—then and now—that salvation only comes through Christ.

They invite us to ask ourselves: if prophets could trust in a Savior they’d never seen, what does that mean for those of us who know His story now? The Book of Mormon’s messianic prophecies bridge the gap between ancient hope and present faith. They remind us that Christ’s promises are just as real today as they were back then.
