 So, how do you actually break these self-defeating habits? Here are a few practical strategies:

 Letting go of self-pity and blame isn’t a single decision—it’s a daily practice. But over time, shifting your focus this way builds a rock-solid foundation for resilience. And when the next storm comes along, you’ll find it’s a lot easier to face it without losing hope or direction.
