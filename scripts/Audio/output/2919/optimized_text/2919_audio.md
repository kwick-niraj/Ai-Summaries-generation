## Chapter 3 Tidy by Category, Not by Location—A Systematic Approach to Decluttering

Chapter 3: Tidy by Category, Not by Location—A Systematic Approach to Decluttering

 

 Have you ever started decluttering one room or maybe just a single drawer, only to realize later that the clutter seems to migrate somewhere else? It’s such a common trap. When you tidy by location, you’re often just shuffling things around. Why does this happen? Well, items of the same kind are usually scattered all over your home. So, you never really get a clear sense of what you own—and it becomes tough to make solid decisions.

 

 Here’s a smarter way: tidy by category. Instead of cleaning one space at a time, you gather every single item of a certain type—let’s say, all your clothes—from any room, closet, or forgotten box. Line them up together, and suddenly, you see the whole story. You can compare, choose, and decide what genuinely serves you. It’s a total game-changer.

 

 The process follows a specific order, too. You start with categories that are easiest to let go of, and gradually work up to the ones that tug at your heartstrings. This way, you build confidence and decision-making skills before you face the really tough stuff. So, what’s the recommended order?

 

 First, begin with clothing. It’s usually less emotional and easier to evaluate. Next, move on to books. Sure, they might hold some memories, but most are still pretty straightforward to sort. Then, tackle papers—think documents, receipts, and all those notes piling up. You’ll be amazed at how much of it can go.

 

 After that comes miscellaneous items—this is what’s called komono. It covers everything from kitchenware to electronics and even toiletries. Finally, save sentimental items for last. These are your mementos, photos, and keepsakes—the things that really require a calm, clear mindset.

 

 By sticking to this sequence, you keep things manageable. Each category gets your full attention, so nothing falls through the cracks. It’s not just about sorting stuff; it’s about making lasting changes.

 

 Within each group, there’s one golden rule: the joy principle. Pick up every item, one by one, and ask yourself—does this spark joy? Or does it truly serve a purpose in your life? If not, it’s time to let go. This keeps you from just moving things from one place to another without actually making progress.

 

 Here are a few practical tips to make this work:

 

 First, gather all items of the chosen category in one spot before you start sorting. Then, work through them individually, always applying the joy test. And most importantly, finish sorting one category completely before you move on to the next.

 

 This systematic approach does more than just organize your home. It reveals patterns—like how many nearly identical shirts you might own or just how many kitchen gadgets you’ve collected over time. It’s often eye-opening, and it encourages you to shop more mindfully in the future.

 

 Tidying by category turns decluttering from a never-ending chore into a focused, purposeful event. You’ll feel a real sense of momentum and accomplishment, and you’ll find it so much easier to keep things tidy for good.
