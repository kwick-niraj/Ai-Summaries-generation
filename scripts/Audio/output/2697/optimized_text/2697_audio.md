And when forests that used to soak up carbon are cut down, the problem just grows. The result? That “blanket” around our planet is getting thicker, locking in more heat, and pushing global temperatures higher and higher.
 
 Scientists have been watching all this unfold for decades. They measure how much carbon dioxide is in the air, and the trend is crystal clear.
