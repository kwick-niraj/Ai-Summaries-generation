 Selling indulgences started to turn the demanding, rewarding journey of repentance into something that looked a lot like a simple financial transaction. 
 
 People began to think they could just pay off their spiritual debts instead of actually feeling remorse or wanting to grow.

 This whole perspective calls us back to the basics of Christian teaching. Repentance isn’t about paying up. It’s an invitation to true transformation—a lifelong journey full of humility, reflection, and a willingness to seek forgiveness, not from people, but from God.
 
 When indulgences offer an easy shortcut, they can actually undermine this essential process.
 
 So, what’s the takeaway here? Selling indulgences really forces us to ask the big questions about what repentance and forgiveness mean. 
 
 True spiritual renewal can’t be bought or sold. It has to be lived, day by day, in every choice and attitude.
