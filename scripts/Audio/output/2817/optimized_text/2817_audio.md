 Chasing profit became the name of the game. Every choice—from what went into the food, to how it was packaged and displayed—was designed to increase sales. Companies learned to tap into our impulses, using everything from clever packaging to strategic shelf placement.

The rise of salt, sugar, and fat wasn’t just about taste—it was a deliberate business move, one that put growth ahead of health.
