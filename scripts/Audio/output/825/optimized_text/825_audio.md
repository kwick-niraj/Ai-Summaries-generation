 Think of this like a loop: you run into a challenge, you fall into a familiar pattern, you feel better—at least for a short while—and then the challenge comes back around. Over and over. The pattern sticks until you spot and address the real payoff hiding underneath.

When you start seeing this trade-off for what it is, you can make more thoughtful choices about when—and how—you seek validation.

 Breaking out of unhelpful patterns isn’t just about gritting your teeth and using willpower.
