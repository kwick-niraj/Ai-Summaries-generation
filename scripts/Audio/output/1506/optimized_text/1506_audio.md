 Or maybe it's a Saturday, and your Highlight turns into finally finishing that home project you've been putting off, or just enjoying an uninterrupted afternoon with your kids.
 
 Once you've picked your Highlight, organize your day around it. 
 That doesn't mean you ignore everything else, but it does mean you give your Highlight a real block of dedicated time and attention.
 Even when life feels hectic, carving out space for this one thing can give you a sense of control, and real accomplishment.
 
 Notice how this approach is different from the usual productivity advice that tells you to do more, faster.

 In the morning, pause for a moment and choose your Highlight.

 And at the end of the day, check in—did you get to your Highlight? How did it make you feel?
 
 This little routine turns your Highlight from a vague wish into a real part of your day.
 Plus, it gives you a way to notice what’s working and what’s not, so you can tweak things as you go.
 If certain Highlights keep making your day better, make them a habit. If you keep running into roadblocks, you'll know what to change next time.
 
 The real magic of the Highlight is in its simplicity. 
 When you focus on just one meaningful thing each day, you make steady progress on what matters most, even when the world around you feels chaotic.
