## Introduction

Have you ever found yourself wondering what it really means to know the divine? Sometimes, those big questions about God’s nature and character spark something deep inside us—a mix of curiosity, awe, and maybe even a little bit of longing. 
 Together, we’re about to dive into an exploration inspired by A.W. Tozer’s classic, **The Knowledge of the Holy**, where the mystery of God isn’t something distant or unreachable, but an invitation to discover qualities like holiness, wisdom, justice, mercy, and love in a way that feels real and close. 
 These aren’t just lofty ideas—they’re living realities that shape how we see ourselves, each other, and the world around us. 
 As we look at each of these divine attributes, you’ll notice that our words sometimes fall short—language can only go so far. But that’s part of the beauty here: the very act of searching, of reaching for understanding, becomes a kind of worship all its own. 
 What if learning about God could transform not just your thoughts, but your whole life—your sense of wonder, your daily choices, even the way you connect with others? That’s the invitation at the heart of Tozer’s writing. 
 So, as we set out on this journey together, let’s stay open to surprise, to challenge, and to the kind of discovery that moves us beyond just knowing about God—toward really encountering His majesty and beauty. 
 In that light, even the ordinary can become sacred, and each step you take draws you closer to a deeper, more transformative relationship with the divine.
