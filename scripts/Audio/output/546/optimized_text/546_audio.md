Instead of measuring success by how much we use or buy, maybe we should ask: are people’s needs being met in a way that can last?

 The way forward isn’t about rejecting technology or turning back the clock. When we start seeing things this way, the idea of “enough” becomes more valuable than always wanting “more.” And with that shift, the future really does start to look a little brighter.
