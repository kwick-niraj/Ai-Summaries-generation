In those moments, the line between what we intended and what the machine actually does gets blurry—and suddenly, control feels a lot further away.
 
 It’s easy to think these are just technical issues for engineers to sort out. But really, all of us have a stake in how artificial intelligence learns and behaves.  Sometimes, it’s worth pausing and asking: what kind of world are we building for the next generation? Will artificial intelligence help us amplify our best qualities—empathy, creativity, wisdom?  The risks are real, and they’re only getting bigger. **But** so is the opportunity to change course. It starts with recognizing that technology isn’t ever truly neutral. It reflects the world we give it. And it’s up to each of us to decide what kind of world that’s going to be.
