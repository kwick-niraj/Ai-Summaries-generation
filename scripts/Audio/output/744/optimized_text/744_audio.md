Instead of being swept away by your thoughts, you learn to stand on the riverbank and watch them flow past.

 Or give **mindful walking** a go. Pay close attention to each step, the movement of your body, and everything around you.
 
 Remember, these practices aren’t about getting rid of tough feelings. They’re about creating space around them—giving you an anchor to return to, even when your mind feels stormy.
