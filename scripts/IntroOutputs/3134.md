**Introduction:**

Assumptions about innate differences between men and women have long shaped expectations, opportunities, and even self-perception. In Delusions of Gender: How Our Minds, Society, and Neurosexism Create Difference, Cordelia Fine unpacks the powerful interplay between neuroscience, culture, and the stories told about gender. The narrative challenges the notion that biology alone determines the abilities, preferences, and roles of men and women, revealing how deeply social influences and unconscious biases shape what is often mistaken for hardwired truth.

Through a critical lens, the world of gender is revealed as a complex landscape where scientific claims and cultural myths intertwine, often reinforcing stereotypes and limiting potential. Fine explores how subtle cues, societal expectations, and the misinterpretation of brain research perpetuate the illusion of fixed gender differences. The journey moves beyond the surface of scientific findings to expose the ways in which neurosexism—biases in the interpretation and communication of neuroscience—can reinforce inequality. This exploration invites a reconsideration of what is possible when the boundaries of gender are seen not as biological destiny, but as constructs open to change and reimagining.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how cultural beliefs, scientific misunderstandings, and unconscious biases shape perceptions of gender. You can expect to gain a deeper understanding of the myths surrounding brain differences, and learn how challenging these assumptions can open doors to greater equality and personal freedom.
