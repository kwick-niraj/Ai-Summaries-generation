**Introduction:**

Happiness often feels elusive, yet some places seem to have discovered its secret recipe. In The Little Book of Lykke: The Danish Search for the World's Happiest People, Meik Wiking delves into the heart of Denmark’s reputation for joy, exploring the cultural habits, social structures, and daily rituals that foster a sense of well-being. The Danish concept of “lykke”—a word that captures the essence of happiness and good fortune—serves as a guiding light, inviting a closer look at how small, intentional choices can transform everyday life. Through stories, research, and a global perspective, the pursuit of happiness becomes not just a personal quest, but a shared journey shaped by community, trust, and kindness.

From cozy gatherings to acts of generosity, the world of lykke reveals that happiness thrives in connection and meaning. Meik Wiking’s exploration moves beyond statistics and rankings, uncovering the subtle, often overlooked moments that make life richer. Whether it’s savoring a meal with friends, cycling through city streets, or finding purpose in helping others, the Danish approach offers practical inspiration for anyone seeking more joy. The search for happiness becomes an invitation to notice, appreciate, and cultivate the small joys that exist all around, setting the stage for a deeper understanding of what it truly means to live well.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover practical ways to infuse more happiness into your daily life, inspired by Danish traditions and global examples. You can expect to gain fresh perspectives on community, gratitude, and well-being, along with actionable ideas to create more meaningful connections and moments of joy.
