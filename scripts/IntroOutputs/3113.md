**Introduction:**

Navigating the evolving landscape of work, organizations face the challenge of fostering collaboration, productivity, and engagement across hybrid and remote teams. The shift away from traditional office environments has redefined how teams connect, communicate, and achieve shared goals. In this new era, questions arise about maintaining trust, ensuring accountability, and leveraging technology to bridge physical distances. The pursuit of competitive advantage now hinges on the ability to adapt leadership styles, implement effective processes, and benchmark against best practices that drive high performance in distributed settings.

Leading Hybrid and Remote Teams by Gleb Tsipursky delves into the strategies and frameworks that empower leaders to thrive amid these changes. Emphasizing evidence-based approaches, the focus turns to practical solutions for overcoming common pitfalls, building resilient team cultures, and aligning remote work structures with organizational objectives. The journey unfolds through actionable insights, real-world examples, and a clear roadmap for benchmarking success. As the boundaries of the workplace continue to blur, the path forward lies in mastering the art of leading teams that are both physically apart and deeply connected in purpose.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn how to benchmark your team’s practices against proven standards, adopt effective leadership strategies for hybrid and remote environments, and foster a culture of trust and accountability. You can expect to gain practical tools for enhancing collaboration, productivity, and competitive advantage in a distributed workforce.
