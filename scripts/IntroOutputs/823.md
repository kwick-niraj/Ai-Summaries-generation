**Introduction:**

Uncertainty shapes every decision, from the smallest daily choices to the most significant life-changing moves. The world rarely offers perfect information, yet outcomes still demand action. In this landscape, the difference between luck and skill, between a good decision and a good result, often blurs. Thinking in Bets: Making Smarter Decisions When You Don't Have All the Facts by Annie Duke explores how embracing uncertainty and probability can transform the way choices are made, both personally and professionally. The mindset of a professional poker player—where every hand is a blend of skill, chance, and incomplete information—becomes a powerful metaphor for navigating the unpredictability of real life.

By shifting focus from the outcome to the quality of the decision-making process, a new framework emerges—one that values learning, adaptability, and resilience over the illusion of certainty. The journey through Annie Duke’s insights reveals how to separate results from decisions, how to recognize and manage cognitive biases, and how to cultivate a habit of thinking probabilistically. This approach not only sharpens judgment but also fosters a culture of constructive feedback and continuous improvement, setting the stage for smarter, more confident choices in an uncertain world.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn how to make better decisions by thinking in probabilities, embracing uncertainty, and focusing on the quality of your choices rather than just the outcomes. You can expect to gain practical tools for managing risk, reducing bias, and building a more resilient, adaptable mindset.
