**Introduction:**

Pain and joy often exist side by side, shaping the landscape of daily life in ways both subtle and profound. The journey from emotional wounds to genuine happiness is rarely straightforward, marked by setbacks, revelations, and the slow rebuilding of trust in oneself and the world. In From Hurt to Happiness: Emotional Rescue from the Ground Up by Mr Mike Van, the transformative power of healing takes center stage, inviting a closer look at how suffering can become a catalyst for growth. The process of moving beyond hurt is not about erasing the past, but about learning to carry it differently—finding strength in vulnerability and hope in the midst of struggle.

As the layers of pain are gently uncovered, new pathways to happiness begin to emerge, grounded in self-awareness, compassion, and resilience. The exploration of emotional rescue unfolds as a practical and deeply personal endeavor, where small shifts in perspective can lead to profound changes in well-being. The narrative weaves together insights on overcoming adversity, building emotional intelligence, and nurturing a sense of inner peace. Through this lens, happiness is revealed not as a distant goal, but as a state cultivated from the ground up, rooted in the courage to face hurt and the wisdom to embrace healing.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn how to transform emotional pain into personal growth, develop practical tools for healing, and cultivate a deeper sense of happiness and resilience. You can expect to gain insights into emotional intelligence, self-compassion, and strategies for building lasting well-being from the inside out.
