**Introduction:**

The search for understanding the divine often leads to profound questions about the nature and character of God. In The Knowledge of the Holy by A.W. Tozer, the exploration centers on the attributes that define the infinite, eternal, and unchanging presence at the heart of Christian faith. Concepts such as holiness, wisdom, justice, mercy, and love are not merely abstract qualities but living realities that shape the relationship between humanity and the divine. The journey into these attributes invites a deeper reverence, challenging assumptions and expanding the sense of awe that surrounds the mystery of God.

As each attribute unfolds, the limitations of human language and understanding become apparent, yet the pursuit itself becomes an act of worship. The Knowledge of the Holy draws attention to the ways in which a true vision of God transforms every aspect of life, from personal devotion to the broader experience of community and worship. The call is not only to know about God but to encounter the majesty and beauty that inspire humility and trust. Through this lens, the ordinary becomes sacred, and the heart is drawn toward a more intimate and transformative relationship with the divine, setting the stage for a lifelong journey of discovery.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will gain a deeper understanding of the attributes of God, leading to a richer sense of awe, reverence, and intimacy in your spiritual life. Expect to discover new perspectives on faith, worship, and the transformative power of truly knowing the divine.
