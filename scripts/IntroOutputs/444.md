**Introduction:**

The journey of parenthood is filled with both profound joy and deep concern, as the desire to nurture, protect, and guide a child shapes every decision and hope. In The Power of a Praying Parent by Stormie Omartian, the transformative role of prayer emerges as a steady anchor amid the uncertainties and challenges of raising children. The act of entrusting a child’s well-being, character, and future to a higher power becomes a source of strength, offering reassurance that no parent is ever truly alone in their efforts. Through heartfelt intercession, the invisible threads of faith and love weave a protective covering over a child’s life, inviting peace into the often unpredictable world of parenting.

As the seasons of childhood unfold, new questions and worries arise—about health, friendships, choices, and the shaping of values. The Power of a Praying Parent gently explores how intentional, faith-filled prayer can address these concerns, fostering a sense of hope and resilience in both parent and child. The practice of lifting up every aspect of a child’s life—emotions, relationships, growth, and dreams—becomes a daily act of devotion, inviting divine guidance and wisdom. In this way, the spiritual journey of parenting is enriched, offering a path toward deeper connection, trust, and lasting peace, even in the midst of life’s uncertainties.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover practical ways to pray for your child’s needs, growth, and protection, while gaining renewed confidence in your role as a parent. You can expect to find encouragement, spiritual insight, and a sense of peace as you learn to entrust your child’s journey to a higher power.
