**Introduction:**

A sharp wind of cultural reckoning sweeps through the landscape, challenging the stories that have long shaped collective beliefs about power, gender, and truth. In The Witches Are Coming, Lindy West confronts the myths and narratives that have allowed misogyny, bigotry, and denialism to flourish in modern society. The specter of the "witch hunt" is reclaimed, not as a tool of oppression, but as a rallying cry for those who refuse to be silenced or gaslit by the status quo. Through incisive wit and unflinching honesty, the conversation turns to the ways in which pop culture, politics, and media have conspired to obscure reality and perpetuate injustice.

As the threads of outrage, humor, and vulnerability intertwine, a tapestry emerges that exposes the absurdities and dangers of dismissing women's voices and experiences. The world is seen through a lens that refuses to accept easy answers or comfortable lies, instead demanding accountability and change. With each argument and anecdote, the call grows louder for a society that recognizes the harm in ignoring inconvenient truths. The journey through these ideas invites a deeper understanding of how cultural forces shape perceptions and the urgent need to challenge them.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how cultural myths and media narratives shape attitudes toward gender, power, and truth. You can expect to gain a sharper awareness of the forces that perpetuate injustice, along with the tools and confidence to challenge harmful norms and demand accountability in your own world.
