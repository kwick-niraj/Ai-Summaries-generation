**Introduction:**

Curiosity about death often lingers at the edges of daily life, surfacing in moments of wonder, discomfort, or even humor. Will My Cat Eat My Eyeballs? And Other Questions About Dead Bodies by Caitlin Doughty delves into the strange, fascinating, and sometimes unsettling realities that surround mortality. The mysteries of what happens to bodies after death, the rituals and taboos that shape our responses, and the quirky, sometimes macabre questions that children and adults alike dare to ask all come together in a candid exploration of the end of life. Through a lens that is both scientific and deeply human, the boundaries between fear and fascination blur, inviting a more open conversation about what it means to die.

From the practicalities of decomposition to the oddities of funeral customs, the journey through these questions reveals not only the biological truths of death but also the cultural and emotional landscapes that frame our understanding. The world of mortuary science, as seen through Doughty’s eyes, becomes a place of learning, empathy, and even laughter, where no question is too strange and no curiosity is dismissed. As the veil lifts on the unknown, a sense of connection emerges—between the living and the dead, between knowledge and myth—offering a fresh perspective on one of life’s most universal experiences.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn surprising facts about death, decomposition, and funeral practices, gaining clarity on questions often left unspoken. You can expect to gain a more comfortable, informed perspective on mortality, as well as a sense of humor and curiosity about the realities that surround the end of life.
