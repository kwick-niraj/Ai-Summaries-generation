**Introduction:**

In the dynamic world of leadership, the difference between a thriving team and a stagnant one often comes down to the daily habits and mindsets of those at the helm. The journey through Lift: Five Practices Great Managers Do Consistently by Raymond Wheeler reveals how exceptional managers elevate both performance and morale, creating environments where employees not only meet expectations but exceed them. Central to this exploration is the understanding that great management is not a matter of innate talent, but of consistent, intentional practices that foster trust, engagement, and growth.

As the narrative unfolds, the focus sharpens on five core practices that set outstanding managers apart. These practices are not abstract theories, but actionable approaches that can be woven into the fabric of any workplace. Through real-world examples and practical insights, the path to becoming a manager who inspires, motivates, and empowers becomes clear. The emphasis remains on the transformative power of consistency—how small, repeated actions can lead to remarkable results, both for individuals and the teams they lead. With this foundation, the stage is set for a deeper dive into the principles that drive high-performing, fulfilled teams.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn how to consistently apply five key management practices that raise both performance and morale, discover actionable strategies to help your employees thrive, and gain insights that empower you to build a more engaged, motivated, and high-achieving team.
