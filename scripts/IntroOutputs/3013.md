**Introduction:**

Deeply held beliefs often shape the way the world is perceived, guiding choices and coloring experiences, yet many of these convictions rest on unexamined assumptions. Hoodwinked: Uncovering Our Fundamental Superstitions by Larry Gottlieb delves into the subtle, pervasive myths that quietly influence daily life, challenging the very foundations of what is accepted as truth. The journey begins with a probing look at the origins of these superstitions—ideas so ingrained that they masquerade as common sense—inviting a reconsideration of the stories told about reality, self, and society.

As the layers of inherited wisdom are peeled back, familiar certainties give way to a landscape of possibility and self-inquiry. The exploration moves beyond mere skepticism, encouraging a deeper awareness of how unquestioned beliefs shape identity, relationships, and the broader human experience. Through this process, the opportunity arises to break free from limiting patterns and discover a more authentic, liberated way of engaging with the world, setting the stage for a profound transformation in understanding and perspective.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn to identify and question the hidden assumptions that shape your worldview, gaining tools to break free from limiting beliefs. You can expect to gain greater self-awareness, clarity, and the confidence to challenge conventional wisdom, opening the door to more authentic choices and deeper personal freedom.
