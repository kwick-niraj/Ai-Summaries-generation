**Introduction:**

Behind the gleaming facades of museums and universities, a powerful family’s legacy intertwines with the roots of a national crisis. Empire of Pain: The Secret History of the Sackler Dynasty by Patrick Radden Keefe delves into the rise of the Sackler family, whose name became synonymous with philanthropy and prestige, yet whose fortune was built on the controversial pharmaceutical empire behind OxyContin. The story unfolds across generations, revealing how ambition, secrecy, and relentless pursuit of influence shaped not only the family’s destiny but also the trajectory of American medicine and public health. The narrative traces the transformation of a once-respected medical lineage into a symbol of both innovation and moral ambiguity, raising profound questions about responsibility, power, and the true cost of success.

As the tale moves from the early days of Arthur Sackler’s marketing genius to the explosive growth of Purdue Pharma, the intricate web of business decisions, legal battles, and public relations maneuvers comes into focus. The Sacklers’ relentless drive for wealth and recognition is set against the backdrop of a growing opioid epidemic, exposing the complex interplay between corporate ambition and societal harm. Through a meticulous exploration of family dynamics, industry practices, and the far-reaching consequences of unchecked influence, the story invites reflection on the blurred lines between philanthropy and profiteering, and the enduring impact of choices made behind closed doors.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will gain a deeper understanding of how personal ambition, corporate strategy, and family legacy can shape—and sometimes devastate—society. You can expect to discover the hidden forces behind the opioid crisis and develop a more critical perspective on power, accountability, and the true meaning of legacy.
