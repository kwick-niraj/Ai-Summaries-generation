**Introduction:**

Power, tradition, and transformation converge in the world of Empress Dowager Cixi, a woman who rose from the obscurity of imperial concubinage to become the de facto ruler of China during a period of seismic change. The late Qing dynasty, marked by internal strife and external threats, became the stage for Cixi’s remarkable ascent. Her story unfolds against a backdrop of rigid court rituals, foreign incursions, and the slow unraveling of an ancient empire. Through her vision and determination, the boundaries of what was possible for a woman in imperial China were redrawn, and the seeds of modernization were sown in a society long resistant to change.

Jung Chang’s exploration of Cixi’s life reveals a figure both controversial and compelling, whose decisions shaped the fate of millions. Navigating palace intrigue, political upheaval, and the pressures of a rapidly changing world, Cixi steered China through the abolition of foot-binding, the introduction of railways and telegraphs, and the first steps toward constitutional reform. Her legacy, often misunderstood or maligned, emerges as a testament to resilience and adaptability in the face of overwhelming odds. The journey through her reign offers a window into the complexities of power, gender, and progress at a pivotal moment in Chinese history, inviting reflection on the forces that drive transformation.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how one extraordinary woman defied tradition to shape the destiny of a nation, gaining insight into the political, cultural, and social upheavals that defined modern China’s emergence. You can expect to gain a nuanced understanding of leadership, resilience, and the complexities of historical change.
