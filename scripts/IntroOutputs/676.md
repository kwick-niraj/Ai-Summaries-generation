**Introduction:**

Curiosity about what happens after death has lingered at the edges of human consciousness for centuries, fueling both spiritual beliefs and scientific inquiry. The search for evidence of an afterlife often collides with the rigor of scientific skepticism, creating a fascinating tension between hope and proof. In Spook: Science Tackles the Afterlife, Mary Roach delves into this intersection, exploring the lengths to which researchers, mediums, and skeptics have gone to answer the ultimate question: is there life after death? The journey traverses haunted laboratories, séances, and the archives of psychic research, where the boundaries between the known and the mysterious are constantly tested.

As the investigation unfolds, the world of Spook reveals a tapestry of experiments, historical oddities, and earnest attempts to measure the unmeasurable. From Victorian-era spirit photography to modern-day studies of near-death experiences, the quest for evidence is both earnest and, at times, delightfully bizarre. The narrative weaves together scientific rigor, human longing, and the occasional dose of humor, inviting a closer look at the ways people have tried to bridge the gap between this world and whatever might lie beyond. The exploration continues, blending skepticism and wonder in the ongoing search for answers.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how science approaches the mysteries of the afterlife, gain insight into the history and methods of paranormal research, and develop a nuanced perspective on the interplay between belief, skepticism, and the enduring human fascination with what comes after death.
