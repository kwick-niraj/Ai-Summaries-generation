**Introduction:**

A century-long battle has shaped societies, policies, and countless lives, all in the name of eradicating drugs. Chasing the Scream: The First and Last Days of the War on Drugs by Johann Hari delves into the tangled roots and far-reaching consequences of this global campaign. The story unfolds through the lives of those caught in the crossfire—addicts, law enforcers, dealers, and reformers—revealing the human cost behind the headlines and statistics. The war on drugs, often painted in stark terms of good and evil, emerges as a complex, deeply human struggle, where pain, hope, and resilience intertwine.

As the narrative moves from the origins of prohibition to the present day, a tapestry of personal stories and historical events comes alive. The relentless pursuit of a drug-free world is contrasted with the unintended consequences: mass incarceration, broken families, and the rise of powerful criminal networks. Through these interconnected journeys, the search for understanding and compassion takes center stage, challenging long-held beliefs and inviting a reexamination of justice, addiction, and healing. The journey through these stories opens the door to new perspectives on what it means to fight, and perhaps end, a war that has touched every corner of society.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will gain a deeper understanding of the origins and impact of the war on drugs, uncovering the personal stories behind policy decisions. You can expect to challenge your assumptions about addiction, justice, and recovery, and discover new perspectives on how societies might move toward more compassionate and effective solutions.
