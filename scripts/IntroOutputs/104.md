**Introduction:**

The intricate dance between intuition and logic shapes every decision, from the mundane to the monumental. In the world of The Undoing Project: A Friendship That Changed Our Minds by Michael Lewis, the boundaries of human judgment are explored through the remarkable partnership of two Israeli psychologists, Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky. Their collaboration delves into the quirks and patterns of the human mind, revealing how biases, heuristics, and emotional influences often override rational thought. The story unfolds against the backdrop of their friendship, a bond that not only fueled groundbreaking discoveries but also redefined the understanding of how people think, choose, and err.

As their ideas ripple outward, the impact is felt far beyond academia, touching fields as diverse as economics, medicine, and public policy. The journey through their intellectual breakthroughs uncovers the hidden mechanisms behind decision-making, challenging the assumption that humans are consistently logical actors. The narrative weaves together personal moments, scientific revelations, and the evolution of a partnership that would ultimately transform the way the world perceives the mind’s inner workings. With each insight, the landscape of human thought becomes more nuanced, inviting a deeper appreciation for the complexity and unpredictability of judgment and choice.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how cognitive biases shape everyday decisions, gain insight into the science of human judgment, and learn how groundbreaking psychological research can transform perspectives on thinking, risk, and relationships. Expect to see your own decision-making in a new, more self-aware light.
