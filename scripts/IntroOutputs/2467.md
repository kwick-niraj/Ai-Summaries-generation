Introduction:
The search for meaning and the nature of existence often leads to profound questions about divinity, self, and the universe. In Know that I am God by P. Ritwik, the boundaries between the individual and the infinite begin to blur, inviting a deeper contemplation of what it means to recognize the divine within and around. The journey unfolds through layers of spiritual inquiry, challenging assumptions about identity, purpose, and the ultimate reality that underpins all experience. The presence of the sacred is not distant or abstract, but intimately woven into the fabric of daily life, waiting to be discovered in moments of stillness and reflection.

As the exploration deepens, the interplay between faith, doubt, and revelation becomes a central theme, guiding the seeker toward a more expansive understanding of self and cosmos. The wisdom of ancient traditions and the insights of personal experience converge, offering a path that is both universal and uniquely personal. The invitation is to move beyond surface appearances and habitual thinking, to encounter the mystery that lies at the heart of being. Through this unfolding, the possibility emerges of living with greater awareness, compassion, and connection to the source of all that is.

What You’ll Get Out of This Book
You will discover new perspectives on spirituality, deepen your understanding of the divine, and gain practical insights for integrating a sense of sacredness into everyday life. You can expect to find inspiration for personal growth, inner peace, and a more meaningful connection to yourself and the world.
