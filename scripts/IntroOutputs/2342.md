**Introduction:**

The Pleasures of God by John Piper invites reflection on the profound and often overlooked reality of divine joy. At the heart of existence lies a God who delights in His own glory, whose happiness is not detached or impersonal, but deeply woven into the fabric of creation. The nature of God’s pleasure—what brings Him joy, what He values, and how He expresses delight—shapes the universe and the lives within it. This exploration moves beyond duty and obligation, drawing attention to the beauty and satisfaction found in God’s own enjoyment of being God, and how that joy overflows into the world.

As the meditations unfold, the focus turns to the ways God’s delight influences everything from His love for His Son to His acts of mercy, justice, and grace. The vision expands, revealing how understanding God’s pleasures transforms the way people see worship, obedience, and the pursuit of happiness. The journey through these themes offers a fresh perspective on the relationship between divine joy and human fulfillment, inviting a deeper encounter with the source of all true pleasure.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how God’s own joy shapes the world and your relationship with Him, gaining insight into the nature of divine delight and its impact on worship, purpose, and daily living. Expect to find a richer understanding of what it means to seek happiness in God’s happiness.
