**Introduction:**

The journey through trauma often leaves invisible marks, shaping thoughts, behaviors, and relationships in ways that can be difficult to understand. In What Happened To You?: Conversations on Trauma, Resilience, and Healing by Bruce D. Perry, the focus shifts from asking "What's wrong with you?" to a more compassionate inquiry: "What happened to you?" This subtle change in perspective opens the door to a deeper understanding of how early experiences, especially those involving adversity or neglect, can influence the brain, body, and emotional life. The exploration of trauma is not just about pain, but also about the remarkable capacity for resilience and the pathways toward healing.

Through a blend of scientific insight and human connection, the world of trauma unfolds as a landscape shaped by both vulnerability and strength. The patterns of response to stress, the ways in which the brain adapts, and the importance of supportive relationships all come into focus. Healing emerges not as a solitary journey, but as a process rooted in empathy, understanding, and the power of safe, nurturing connections. The conversation moves beyond diagnosis and labels, inviting a more holistic view of human experience and the potential for growth after hardship.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will gain a deeper understanding of how trauma shapes behavior and development, discover the science behind resilience, and learn practical ways to foster healing in yourself and others. Expect to shift your perspective toward greater empathy, compassion, and hope for recovery and growth.
