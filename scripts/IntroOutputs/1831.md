The wisdom of Chinese astrology becomes a compass for self-care, offering tailored approaches to diet, movement, rest, and emotional balance. As the patterns of the heavens mirror the patterns within, a path emerges toward greater vitality and holistic wellbeing, guided by the timeless connection between the cosmos and the self.
