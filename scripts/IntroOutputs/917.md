**Introduction:**

First impressions often shape the course of relationships, both personal and professional, in ways that linger far beyond the initial encounter. The world of How to Make People Like You in 90 Seconds or Less by Nicholas Boothman delves into the subtle art of connection, revealing how body language, tone, and attitude can open doors to trust and rapport almost instantly. The power of a smile, the warmth of eye contact, and the confidence in a handshake all become tools for building bridges, transforming fleeting moments into lasting bonds. The underlying message is clear: genuine connection is not a matter of luck, but of skill—one that can be learned, practiced, and mastered.

Within this framework, practical strategies emerge for navigating social situations with ease and authenticity. Techniques for reading cues, mirroring behaviors, and finding common ground become second nature, allowing even the most reserved individuals to engage with confidence. The journey unfolds as a series of actionable insights, each designed to break down barriers and foster meaningful interactions. As the process of connecting becomes demystified, the path to influence, collaboration, and friendship grows clearer, inviting a new sense of possibility into every encounter.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn how to quickly build rapport, make memorable first impressions, and communicate with confidence in any situation. You can expect to gain practical techniques for reading body language, initiating conversations, and creating genuine connections that open doors in both your personal and professional life.
