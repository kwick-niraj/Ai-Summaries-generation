**Introduction:**

Questions about the nature of faith and the presence of the divine often intersect with the deepest struggles of the human psyche. In The Right Hand of God: A Psychotherapist's Case for the Reality of the Living God, Ara C. Trembly MS MA LPC explores the profound relationship between psychological healing and spiritual experience. The journey through pain, doubt, and transformation reveals how the search for meaning and connection can lead to encounters with something greater than oneself. The boundaries between science and spirituality blur as the complexities of the mind are examined alongside the enduring human longing for the sacred.

Within this exploration, personal stories and clinical insights intertwine, illuminating the ways in which belief in a living God can shape the process of recovery and growth. The narrative moves through moments of crisis, hope, and revelation, suggesting that the presence of the divine is not merely a matter of doctrine but a lived reality that can be witnessed in the therapeutic encounter. As the layers of psychological distress are peeled back, the possibility emerges that healing is not only a matter of technique, but also of grace. The path forward invites a reconsideration of what it means to be whole, and how the right hand of God might be extended in the midst of human suffering.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how spiritual belief and psychological healing can work together, gaining insights into the ways faith may influence personal growth and recovery. You can expect to gain a deeper understanding of the interplay between mental health and spirituality, and how the presence of the divine might be experienced in everyday life.
