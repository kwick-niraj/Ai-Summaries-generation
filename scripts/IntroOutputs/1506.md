**Introduction:**

Modern life often feels like a relentless race, with endless to-do lists and constant digital distractions pulling attention in every direction. The sense of busyness can be overwhelming, leaving little room for the things that truly matter. In Make Time: How to Focus on What Matters Every Day by Jake Knapp, the spotlight turns to the art of reclaiming control over daily routines and finding clarity amid the noise. The struggle to prioritize meaningful work and personal fulfillment becomes a central theme, inviting a closer look at how small, intentional changes can transform the experience of each day.

Within this world, the pursuit of focus and presence takes center stage, offering practical strategies for breaking free from the default mode of distraction. The journey unfolds through a series of actionable ideas designed to help carve out time for what is most important, whether that means creative projects, relationships, or personal well-being. As the rhythm of daily life shifts from reactive to purposeful, a new sense of agency emerges, setting the stage for lasting change and a deeper connection to what truly matters.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will learn how to identify and prioritize what’s most important in your day, develop habits that protect your focus, and discover practical techniques to minimize distractions. You can expect to gain a renewed sense of control over your time and a toolkit for making each day more intentional and fulfilling.
