**Introduction:**

Generations intertwine, carrying the weight of untold stories and inherited dreams, as the search for identity unfolds against the backdrop of family history. In We Are Bridges: A Memoir by Cassandra Lane, the journey of self-discovery is inseparable from the legacy of ancestors whose lives were shaped by love, loss, and the enduring scars of racial violence. The echoes of the past reverberate through the present, inviting reflection on how personal and collective histories shape the very core of belonging and resilience.

Through the lens of memory and imagination, the narrative weaves together the fragments of a family’s lineage, tracing the impact of trauma and hope across time. The longing to understand one’s place in the world becomes a powerful force, compelling a deeper exploration of heritage, motherhood, and the act of bearing witness. As the threads of past and present converge, the story reveals the transformative power of reclaiming history, forging connection, and finding healing in the act of remembrance.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how personal and ancestral histories shape identity, gain insight into the complexities of generational trauma, and find inspiration in the resilience that emerges from confronting the past. You can expect to reflect on your own roots and the bridges built between memory, healing, and hope.
