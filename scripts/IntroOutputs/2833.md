**Introduction:**

For centuries, the challenge of determining a ship’s longitude at sea haunted explorers, scientists, and monarchs alike, as lives and fortunes depended on accurate navigation. The world’s oceans became both a highway for discovery and a graveyard for those lost to miscalculation, with the inability to pinpoint east-west position leading to disaster after disaster. Amid this backdrop of urgency and ambition, the quest for a solution became one of the most pressing scientific problems of the age, drawing in the greatest minds and sparking fierce competition across continents.

Longitude: The True Story of a Lone Genius Who Solved the Greatest Scientific Problem of His Time by Dava Sobel brings to life the remarkable journey of John Harrison, a self-taught clockmaker whose relentless ingenuity defied the odds. As the story unfolds, the drama of invention, rivalry, and perseverance reveals how a single individual’s vision and determination could reshape the fate of nations. The narrative traces the evolution of Harrison’s revolutionary marine chronometer, the skepticism he faced, and the ultimate triumph that changed navigation forever, inviting reflection on the power of human creativity and the enduring impact of one person’s quest.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how a single breakthrough can alter the course of history, gain insight into the intersection of science, perseverance, and innovation, and come away with a deeper appreciation for the human drive to solve seemingly insurmountable problems. Expect to be inspired by the story of determination and ingenuity.
