**Introduction:**

Desires shape destinies, often more powerfully than conscious beliefs or intellectual convictions. Beneath the surface of daily routines, habits quietly mold the heart, steering affections and aspirations in directions that may go unnoticed. In You Are What You Love: The Spiritual Power of Habit, James K.A. Smith delves into the profound connection between spiritual formation and the rhythms of everyday life, revealing how the things people repeatedly do are not just actions, but formative practices that sculpt the very core of identity. The question shifts from "What do you think?" to "What do you love?"—inviting a deeper exploration of how love, more than knowledge, directs the course of a life.

Within this framework, the ordinary becomes sacred, and the mundane routines of work, worship, and relationships are recast as powerful liturgies that shape the soul. Smith’s insights illuminate the subtle ways in which culture, community, and personal rituals influence what is truly valued, often beneath the level of conscious choice. The journey unfolds as an invitation to reexamine the habits that guide daily existence, offering a vision of spiritual growth rooted not in mere willpower, but in the intentional cultivation of love and desire. This perspective opens a path toward transformation, where the alignment of heart and habit becomes the foundation for a flourishing, purpose-driven life.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover how everyday habits and routines profoundly shape your desires, identity, and spiritual life. You can expect to gain practical insights for reorienting your heart and actions toward what truly matters, empowering you to cultivate deeper purpose, intentionality, and spiritual growth in daily living.
