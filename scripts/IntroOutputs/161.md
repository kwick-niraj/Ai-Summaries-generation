**Introduction:**

Identity, trauma, and resilience intertwine in the world of Know My Name by Chanel Miller, where the aftermath of sexual assault is explored with unflinching honesty and profound insight. The journey begins with a young woman’s life forever altered by violence, but it quickly expands into a powerful meditation on the complexities of memory, the weight of shame, and the struggle to reclaim one’s voice in a society that too often silences survivors. The narrative pulses with the tension between vulnerability and strength, revealing how the search for justice can become a crucible for self-discovery and transformation.

As the story unfolds, the personal becomes universal, illuminating the hidden costs of trauma and the courage required to confront both external judgment and internalized doubt. The experience of navigating the legal system, the media’s gaze, and the expectations of family and community is rendered with clarity and empathy, inviting reflection on the broader cultural forces that shape the experience of survivors everywhere. Through pain and perseverance, a new sense of self emerges—one that refuses to be defined by victimhood alone, but instead claims the right to be seen, heard, and known. The path forward is neither simple nor linear, but it is marked by a growing sense of agency and hope.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will gain a deeper understanding of the emotional and societal impact of sexual assault, discover the power of reclaiming your own story, and find inspiration in the resilience required to heal and advocate for change. You can expect to develop greater empathy and awareness around issues of trauma and justice.
