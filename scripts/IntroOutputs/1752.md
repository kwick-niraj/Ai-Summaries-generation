**Introduction:**

Displacement, survival, and the search for belonging shape the journey through a world upended by war. Into Exile by Elin Toona Gottschalk traces the harrowing path of a young girl and her family as they are forced to flee their homeland in the wake of Soviet occupation. The trauma of sudden loss, the uncertainty of the refugee experience, and the resilience required to navigate unfamiliar lands all come to the fore, revealing the profound costs of conflict on both individual and collective identity. The story unfolds against the backdrop of World War II’s chaos, where the boundaries between home and exile blur, and the meaning of safety is constantly redefined.

As the narrative moves from the familiar landscapes of Estonia to the unknown territories of Western Europe, the emotional landscape shifts as well—grief, hope, and determination intertwine. The struggle to adapt, the longing for lost roots, and the small moments of kindness encountered along the way illuminate the complexity of exile. Through the eyes of a child, the world is both threatening and full of possibility, and the journey becomes not just a physical escape, but a profound transformation of self. The experience of exile, as seen through Gottschalk’s lens, becomes a testament to the enduring strength of the human spirit, setting the stage for a deeper exploration of memory, identity, and the meaning of home.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will gain a deeper understanding of the refugee experience, the emotional toll of displacement, and the resilience required to rebuild a life in exile. You can expect to discover new perspectives on identity, belonging, and the enduring impact of war on individuals and families.
