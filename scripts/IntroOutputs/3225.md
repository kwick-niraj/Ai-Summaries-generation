**Introduction:**
The promise of gold stars, high grades, and incentive plans often seems like a straightforward path to motivation and achievement. Yet beneath the surface, these familiar rewards can quietly undermine genuine interest, creativity, and long-term growth. In Punished by Rewards, Alfie Kohn challenges the deeply held belief that external motivators—whether in classrooms, workplaces, or homes—lead to better performance and happier individuals. Instead, the world of rewards reveals a paradox: the more people are bribed to do something, the less they may care about the activity itself, and the more fragile their motivation becomes.

Exploring the subtle dynamics of praise, incentives, and behavioral control, Kohn uncovers how these strategies can erode intrinsic motivation and foster compliance rather than true engagement. The journey through his insights reveals the hidden costs of relying on external validation, inviting a reimagining of how people learn, work, and relate to one another. As the layers of reward-based systems are peeled back, a new vision emerges—one that values autonomy, curiosity, and authentic connection over manipulation and short-term gains.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**
You will discover why common reward systems often backfire, learn how to foster genuine motivation in yourself and others, and gain practical strategies for encouraging deeper engagement, creativity, and meaningful achievement without relying on bribes or external incentives.
