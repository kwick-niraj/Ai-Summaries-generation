**Introduction:**

Wit and irreverence collide in a world where everyday absurdities and human quirks are laid bare. Nobody Asked Me, But .... by Karl Wiggins delves into the unpredictable terrain of modern life, where opinions flow freely and the boundaries between humor and honesty blur. Through a lens that is both sharp and playful, the narrative explores the peculiarities of social conventions, the oddities of human behavior, and the unfiltered thoughts that often remain unspoken. The result is a tapestry of observations that challenge the status quo, inviting a closer look at the things people take for granted or overlook entirely.

As the journey unfolds, a mosaic of anecdotes, rants, and reflections emerges, each piece colored by a distinctive voice that refuses to shy away from the uncomfortable or the hilarious. The world is dissected with a blend of satire and sincerity, offering a fresh perspective on topics ranging from the mundane to the profound. With each turn, the familiar becomes strange and the strange becomes familiar, creating a space where laughter and insight coexist. The experience is both entertaining and thought-provoking, setting the stage for a deeper exploration of the everyday moments that shape lives and attitudes.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover a refreshing perspective on the quirks of daily life, gain insight into unconventional viewpoints, and find humor in unexpected places. You can expect to be both entertained and challenged to see the world through a more candid, irreverent lens.
