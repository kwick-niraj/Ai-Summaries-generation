**Introduction:**

Curiosity about what happens to the human body after death has lingered for centuries, blending fascination, reverence, and a touch of the macabre. In Stiff: The Curious Lives of Human Cadavers, Mary Roach delves into the remarkable and often surprising roles that human bodies play after life ends. From the silent contributions to medical science and forensic research to the unexpected ways cadavers have shaped history, the journey of the body after death is revealed as both practical and profound. The boundaries between science, ethics, and human emotion blur, inviting a deeper reflection on mortality and the enduring impact of the physical self.

Exploring the many paths a cadaver might take—whether advancing surgical techniques, aiding criminal investigations, or even contributing to crash test safety—unveils a world where death is not an end, but a transformation into new forms of service. The narrative moves through laboratories, classrooms, and even unusual historical anecdotes, uncovering the dignity, humor, and humanity that persist beyond the grave. With each chapter, the story of the human body after death becomes a testament to the ongoing relationship between the living and the departed, setting the stage for a journey that is as enlightening as it is unexpected.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will discover the surprising ways human bodies continue to serve science, medicine, and society after death. You can expect to gain a new perspective on mortality, an appreciation for the contributions of cadavers, and a deeper understanding of the ethical and emotional complexities surrounding the end of life.
