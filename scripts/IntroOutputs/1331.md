**Introduction:**

A world shaped by uncertainty and hope unfolds through the journey of a young boy forced to leave everything behind. In the Sea There are Crocodiles: Based on the True Story of Enaiatollah Akbari by Fabio Geda traces the harrowing odyssey of Enaiatollah, a child who escapes the dangers of Afghanistan and embarks on a quest for safety across borders and cultures. The story pulses with the raw emotions of displacement, resilience, and the search for belonging, as Enaiatollah navigates a landscape where trust is fragile and survival depends on courage and adaptability.

Each step along Enaiatollah’s path reveals the stark realities faced by countless refugees: the loss of home, the unpredictability of strangers, and the small acts of kindness that become lifelines. The narrative weaves together moments of fear and hope, painting a vivid portrait of a boy’s transformation as he confronts adversity and discovers unexpected strength. Through encounters with smugglers, border guards, and fellow travelers, the journey becomes not just a physical passage, but a profound exploration of identity and humanity. The story’s momentum carries forward, inviting reflection on the meaning of home and the enduring power of hope.

**What You’ll Get Out of This Book**

You will gain a deeper understanding of the refugee experience, witnessing the emotional and practical challenges faced by those forced to flee their homes. You can expect to develop empathy, insight into resilience, and a renewed appreciation for the strength found in hope and human connection.
