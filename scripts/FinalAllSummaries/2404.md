## Chapter 1 Hearing the Father’s Call: Rediscovering God’s Heart

For many, the idea of God the Father is just that—present in prayers, mentioned in creeds, but rarely approached with the warmth and intimacy reserved for other figures in faith. The journey into devotion to the Divine Heart of God the Father begins with this sense of rediscovery, a gentle invitation to see the Father not as a distant authority, but as the source of all tenderness, mercy, and love.

It often starts quietly. Someone might be sitting in a church pew, distracted by the worries of the week, when a sudden sense of longing arises—a desire for peace, for protection, for a love that feels both strong and safe. In these moments, the heart aches for something more than routine. The book’s story unfolds from such moments, where the ordinary is interrupted by a sense of the extraordinary presence of the Father.

The devotion described here is not a new religion or a replacement for what has come before. Instead, it’s a return to the roots of Christian faith, where God the Father is recognized as a living, loving Person, not just a theological concept. The narrative draws from private revelations—those deeply personal experiences where the Father’s love is felt as real and immediate. These experiences are not reserved for mystics or saints; they are offered to anyone willing to listen.

It was as if the Father Himself was reaching out, inviting her to rest in His care. This was not a vision or a dramatic miracle, but a subtle shift—a new awareness of the Father’s presence.

The book’s message is clear: the Father longs to be known and loved, not just as part of the Trinity, but as a distinct Person with a Heart overflowing with mercy. In a world marked by anxiety and division, the invitation is simple—come closer, trust more deeply, and allow the Father’s love to heal old wounds.

Many people, even those who pray regularly, carry hidden fears about God. Some see Him as stern or distant, others as unpredictable or demanding. The devotion to the Divine Heart of the Father gently challenges these images. Through stories and prayers, it paints a picture of a Father who is endlessly patient, always ready to forgive, and eager to pour out blessings on His children.

This call to devotion is not just for individuals; it’s a call for the whole world. The narrative describes a vision of the Father’s Heart shining with light, reaching out to every nation and people. The hope is that, by turning to the Father in trust and love, families and communities will find new strength and unity. The journey begins with a simple step: recognizing the Father’s presence and responding to His call. It’s about opening the heart, even just a little, to the possibility that the Father’s love is real, personal, and transformative.
